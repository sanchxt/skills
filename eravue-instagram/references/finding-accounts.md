# Finding accounts worth a DM

## Who to look for (default order)

Owner-run businesses where one new client is worth a lot and where the gap is visible from the profile. Start with niches where Eravue has matching proof (see eravue-profile.md).

## Sweet spot

- **Followers:** about 500 to 50k for local businesses, 5k to 200k for creators. Under ~300 often means no budget yet; over ~200k usually has a team and an agency.
- **Active:** posted in the last 30 days (they care about Instagram), but with a clear gap.
- **Business signals:** a business/creator account category, a city, prices or services mentioned, a phone/WhatsApp/email button, ads in Meta Ad Library, a physical location.
- **Owner-run:** first-person captions, founder's face in reels, "I" not "we the team". These owners reply to DMs themselves.

## Search methods (use several; vary each day)

1. **Instagram keyword search** (logged in): search box → type niche + city ("interior designer gurgaon", "home baker noida", "dentist dubai"). Open the Accounts and Places tabs. Business names in search results are strong candidates.
2. **Hashtags:** `instagram.com/explore/tags/<tag>/`. Use local and niche tags, not huge ones: `#gurgaoninteriors`, `#delhibaker`, `#noidasalon`, `#homebakerdelhi`, `#vastuconsultant`, `#delhidentist`, `#smallbusinessindia`. The top/recent posts lead to businesses.
3. **Location pages:** `instagram.com/explore/locations/...` for a market, mall, or neighbourhood (e.g. DLF CyberHub, Khan Market, Sector 29). Businesses tag their own location.
4. **"Similar accounts"** on a good prospect's profile (the arrow next to Follow). One good find gives 10 more in the same niche and city.
5. **Tagged posts of suppliers and venues:** venues, wedding planners, and malls tag the vendors they work with.
6. **Google:** `site:instagram.com "interior designer" "gurgaon"`, `site:instagram.com "home baker" "noida" "dm to order"`, `site:instagram.com "vastu" "consultation" "dm"`. Good for finding accounts without browsing.
7. **Google Maps → Instagram:** businesses with good reviews but no website often list only an Instagram link. Strong website prospects.
8. **Meta Ad Library** (`facebook.com/ads/library`, country India, category "All ads", search a niche word): advertisers have a budget. Few or stale creatives = ai-content / video-editing angle; ads that drive to DMs = automation angle.
9. **Comments on big local accounts** (city food bloggers, wedding pages): small businesses comment there to get seen. Open the business accounts only, not personal ones.

## Skip

- Personal accounts with no business, minors, students, private accounts.
- Accounts that already have a polished website, fast replies, consistent reels and visible agency credits ("managed by @agency"). Nothing to fix.
- Franchise/brand outlets managed centrally (ask HQ instead, via LinkedIn or email; use eravue-outreach).
- Anything sensitive: health crises, legal trouble, grief, adult, gambling, crypto pumps, MLM.
- Accounts already in the tracker (dedupe with `check`) unless a follow-up is due.
- Accounts whose DMs are closed (no Message option) → note the email/phone for eravue-outreach instead.

## Pace while browsing

Open profiles one at a time, read, move on, like a person would. No scripted scrolling loops, no collecting follower lists, no opening dozens of tabs at once. About 10 good leads usually needs 30-60 profile visits; that's fine.
