# Creative taste and anti-patterns

This file records explicit user feedback about creative direction. It should guide ideation and scripting, but it is not proof that any format will perform. Update it only from explicit user feedback or meaningful account results; do not infer durable taste from silence.

## Current preference signal

Feedback date: 2026-09-06.

The user wants LifeWithCofounders to grow as a broad, entertaining, human-led creator account. The two creators being cofounders is background context and a possible occasional topic, not the default niche. The audience should not need to be founders, agency workers, marketers, clients, or startup enthusiasts.

### Promising directions

- Recognizable current culture, sport, entertainment, internet, or everyday-life topics with a strong execution.
- Real challenges and experiments with visible attempts, stakes, judgment, comparison, or results.
- The two creators' natural contrast used as an entertainment device rather than discussed as “cofounder chemistry.”
- Conversation that creates an open loop and moves quickly into proof, footage, an attempt, a reveal, or an emotional payoff.
- Ideas that can attract broad non-followers first and reveal the creators' personalities during the experience.
- Original dialogue or natural sound when a generic trending song adds little.

### Demonstrated reactions to the prior idea set

- **Messi and Ronaldo potentially playing together:** acceptable as a timely topic, but the user supplied the underlying idea. Do not count the model's fact-checking as original ideation, and do not infer that all news explainers are desired.
- **AI versus human challenge:** considered decent and worth trying. The useful mechanism is a real side-by-side test with a reveal, not the workplace or founder framing.
- **₹500 marketing battle:** may work after modification. The useful mechanism is constrained competition with real stakes and a measurable result; “make the company famous” is too business-led for the default account.

### Rejected or weak directions

Do not default to:

- cofounder report cards, cofounder courts, role swaps, “who is more likely,” or generic banter about working together;
- angry-client replies, client-message guessing, email-tone comparisons, “quick call” jokes, approval problems, or agency-client stereotypes;
- founder expenses, founder tasks, startup advice, old website roasts, browser-history swaps, or business lessons presented as entertainment;
- forced analogies such as turning Messi and Ronaldo into fictional cofounders;
- festival-office arguments or topical hooks whose actual subject is workplace content;
- straight news reporting, cautious fact recitation, or a source summary with two faces added;
- polished copywriting phrases such as “respect was discovered,” “different superpowers,” “brutally honest,” or a neat moral about great partnerships;
- concepts that feel like an HR/LinkedIn sketch, brand campaign exercise, or creator prompt template.

These are taste guardrails, not a ban on every business reference forever. A founder/business story may be used when it is unusually specific, true, broadly understandable, visually demonstrable, and stronger than the available mainstream ideas.

## Tone test

Dialogue should sound like something these two people might actually say in a room. Prefer short interruptions, reactions, incomplete sentences, specific observations, and real stakes. Avoid balanced debate-club dialogue in which each person cleanly states a position for the viewer.

Humor should come from recognition, timing, behavior, surprise, or the actual situation. Do not label ordinary criticism “brutal,” stage resentment, or rely on the audience assuming a romantic relationship.

## Broad-audience test

Before presenting an idea, mentally remove all references to Eravue, founders, cofounders, clients, startups, marketing, and work. If there is no interesting human premise left, it is not a broad reach concept.

The duo should usually be the **hosts of the interesting thing**, not the interesting thing itself.

## Do not overcorrect

- Do not turn the account into a random trend-reposting page.
- Do not pretend the creators are a couple.
- Do not select a niche permanently without enough account data.
- Do not reject every conversational Reel; reject conversations that lack a second entertainment mechanism or payoff.
- Do not assume the three reactions above define all future taste. Use new explicit feedback and performance data to refine this file.
