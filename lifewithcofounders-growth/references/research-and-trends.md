# Research and trends

## Research a timely opportunity

Begin with a concrete date, target region (default India), language, and audience. A trend is useful only if it is current, rising or still culturally alive, relevant to the account, feasible to produce quickly, and transformable into an original two-founder post.

### Discovery lanes

Research several lanes in parallel when tools permit:

1. **Instagram-native:** Reels feed, search suggestions, audio pages and upward-arrow indicators, templates, creator examples, comments, Professional Dashboard Best Practices, saved audio, Edits inspiration, and Trial Reels.
2. **Current culture:** Google Trends, reputable news, sport/entertainment calendars, releases, memes, holidays, and India-specific conversations.
3. **Adjacent creators:** duo/couple comedy, founder creators, work humor, current-affairs explainers, technology/lifestyle creators. Capture structure, not wording.
4. **Cross-platform:** YouTube Shorts and, where accessible, TikTok to see whether the pattern is spreading rather than isolated.
5. **Community evidence:** recent Reddit threads and comments to find pain points, audience language, fatigue, and counterexamples.

Do not rely on search snippets alone when a primary page can be opened. Record publication/post dates. Avoid trend-forecast articles that merely recycle generic lists.

## Trend evidence card

For every recommended trend or audio, report:

```text
Trend/audio:
Observed on:
Evidence date:
Region/language:
Evidence strength: high / medium / exploratory
Lifecycle: emerging / accelerating / mature / fading
Why people participate or share:
LifeWithCofounders transformation:
Production deadline:
Saturation or brand risk:
Sources:
```

High strength normally requires a live Instagram signal plus at least one independent corroboration. Medium can be several recent creator examples or cross-platform movement. Exploratory is a low-cost creative bet, not a “current trend” fact.

## Competitive format audit

Sample recent posts and outliers from at least five relevant accounts when access allows. For each, note:

- date, topic, length, opening frame and first line;
- speaker/character roles and reveal pattern;
- edit rhythm and payoff;
- comments that explain why viewers reacted;
- views relative to that account's recent median, not raw views alone;
- whether a repeatable series or a one-off event drove the result;
- what can be ethically abstracted: question structure, contrast, recurring segment, proof, or transition.

Do not infer causation from one viral Reel. Do not treat follower/engagement figures from influencer databases as exact without visible methodology and a current date.

## Daily idea synthesis

Produce 5–10 candidates across three horizons:

- **Now (24–72h):** time-sensitive event or fast-rising format.
- **This week:** conversation with several days of relevance.
- **Evergreen:** a recurring founder/relationship/work truth.

Score each candidate 0–2 for identity fit, immediate clarity, curiosity/tension, sendability, payoff, and repeatability. Include production effort and expiry. Recommend the top two or three; fully package at least one. Do not dump twenty interchangeable prompts.

## Audio selection

Audio popularity alone is insufficient. Check:

- whether usage is rising rather than merely large;
- whether small and mid-sized creators are participating;
- whether the emotional beat matches the script;
- whether dialogue remains intelligible;
- whether the audio is available for the account type and target region;
- whether commercial usage or cross-posting creates rights restrictions.

Offer three routes: current trend, subtle mood bed, and original audio/no music. Say when the user must verify availability inside Instagram before publishing.

## News and sport content

Verify the claim and event date using authoritative reporting or the official league/team/organization. Separate fact from rumor. The two-founder angle must add perspective, explanation, disagreement, or relatable consequence; reposting highlights with captions is neither sufficiently original nor necessarily licensed.

## If Instagram pages are inaccessible

Say what could not be inspected. Use indexed public evidence only as provisional, and ask for links, screenshots, a screen recording of recent grids/Insights, or an Insights export when precision matters. Do not pretend to have audited posts that were unavailable.
