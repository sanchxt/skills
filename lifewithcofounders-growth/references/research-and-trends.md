# Research and trends

Use this reference for current opportunities, trend/audio requests, calendars containing timely posts, or a user-supplied current claim. Research improves timing and execution; it does not automatically make a headline entertaining.

## Define the research target

Begin with the current date, target region (default India), likely language, production deadline, and any topic the user supplied. Distinguish five signal types:

1. **Event/topic:** sport, entertainment, release, public moment, festival, technology, or cultural conversation.
2. **Native format:** recurring Reel structure, transition, edit pattern, prompt, game, challenge, or visual convention.
3. **Meme/behavior:** recognizable joke, phrase, social behavior, audience debate, or remix pattern.
4. **Audio:** song, speech, sound effect, or timing template whose current use can be verified.
5. **Audience question:** a real curiosity, fantasy, misconception, disagreement, or emotional concern visible in searches or comments.

Do not use “trend” as a synonym for news. A strong daily research set should normally include more than one signal type.

## Discovery order

Use the strongest accessible evidence for the requested decision:

1. Instagram Professional Dashboard/Best Practices, account Insights, Trial Reels, saved audios, Reels trends/inspiration, current audio pages, search suggestions, and live feed examples.
2. Recent original posts from relevant creators and the comments beneath them. Inspect dates and performance relative to each creator's own recent posts when possible.
3. Official event, league, team, artist, studio, product, or platform sources for facts and dates.
4. YouTube Shorts, Google Trends, TikTok when accessible, and other current cultural surfaces for corroboration or migration across platforms.
5. Recent Reddit/community discussion for fatigue, audience language, objections, and ideas; label it anecdotal.
6. Reputable analytics studies with methodology and sample dates for broad context only.

Avoid scraped “top trending sounds” lists, undated compilations, search snippets when the source is available, and raw view comparisons between differently sized accounts.

## From signal to concept

A current signal is raw material. Before recommending it, add an entertainment mechanism and a duo execution.

For each promising signal, ask:

- What do people feel, argue about, hope for, misunderstand, predict, or want to try?
- Can the creators test, guess, compete, recreate, rank, decide, reveal, or personally demonstrate something?
- What changes visually after the first line?
- What payoff exists beyond learning the headline?
- Would the idea still be entertaining after the news is no longer new?

If the answer is only “one creator asks and the other explains,” keep researching or add a second engine.

### User-supplied event or claim

Verify material facts, then create at least three different treatments when the user wants ideation—for example emotional story, prediction/debate, challenge/game, experiment/recreation, visual transition, or personal consequence. Do not count the user's underlying topic as an original idea. Do not turn unrelated celebrities or events into founder/startup analogies.

## Competitive format audit

Use this for strategy work or when the user names creator examples; it is not required for every quick script request. Sample at least five relevant accounts when access permits, drawing from mainstream duo/couple-style creators, comedy, lifestyle, sport/entertainment, experiments/challenges, and adjacent Indian creators rather than mostly founder accounts.

For each account, record:

- post date, topic, length, opening frame, and first spoken/text line;
- entertainment mechanism and visible progression;
- the two participants' roles, if applicable;
- payoff and ending;
- views relative to that account's recent median, when visible;
- comments revealing why people watched, sent, objected, or requested more;
- whether the performance appears tied to a one-off event, a topic, a format, an existing fan base, or an unclear combination;
- what can be ethically abstracted without copying wording, identity, jokes, or footage.

Do not infer causation from a single viral Reel. Do not use a directory's follower or engagement estimate as proof that a format caused growth.

## Trend evidence

Use a compact evidence card for trends that materially affect the recommendation:

```text
Signal:
Type: event/topic | native format | meme/behavior | audio | audience question
Observed on and date:
Region/language:
Evidence strength: high | medium | exploratory
Lifecycle: emerging | accelerating | mature | fading | event-bound
Why people care or participate:
Original LifeWithCofounders execution:
Production deadline:
Saturation, rights, or brand risk:
Sources:
```

High confidence normally requires a live Instagram signal plus independent corroboration. Medium confidence may come from several recent creator examples or strong cross-platform movement. Exploratory means a low-cost test, not a factual claim that something is trending.

## Idea-set synthesis

When asked for a large idea set:

1. Privately generate at least twice the requested number across different topics and mechanisms.
2. Include a mix of timely, this-week, and evergreen ideas, but do not force a calendar horizon when it weakens the set.
3. Ensure the list includes mainstream culture/interest, challenges/experiments, everyday duo/lifestyle, and curiosity/story lanes. Founder/business may occupy at most roughly 10% unless requested.
4. Apply the creative-taste rules and quality gates from `SKILL.md`.
5. Remove concepts that differ only by topic noun or opening wording.
6. Compare survivors and return only those above the quality floor. State when fewer ideas survived.

For each returned concept include:

- one-sentence premise;
- target cold viewer;
- hook or opening visual;
- what visibly happens;
- payoff;
- why someone might send it;
- effort and, if timely, expiry;
- biggest creative risk.

Recommend the top two or three with a short comparative reason. Fully package at least one when useful; do not inflate every candidate with a near-perfect score.

## News, sport, entertainment, and third-party media

Verify claims and event dates through authoritative reporting or official organizations. Separate confirmed fact, report, rumor, prediction, and creator opinion in both script and caption.

Prefer original performance, reenactment, graphics, stills the creators may use, licensed material, or platform-native remix tools. Brief commentary does not automatically make unlicensed highlights safe, and credit is not permission. The third-party clip should support the creators' contribution rather than become the entire value.

## Audio selection

Check:

- whether use appears to be rising rather than merely large;
- evidence date and target region;
- whether small and mid-sized creators are participating;
- whether the sound carries the format or merely fills silence;
- fit with the emotional beat and edit timing;
- dialogue intelligibility;
- availability for the account type and commercial/cross-posting restrictions.

Offer up to three routes when useful: a verified current sound, a subtle mood bed, and original audio/no music. Say when availability must be checked inside Instagram immediately before publishing.

## If Instagram is inaccessible

State exactly what could not be inspected. Use indexed or cross-platform evidence only as provisional and reduce confidence. For account-specific decisions, request post links, screenshots, a screen recording, or an Insights export rather than guessing. Never claim a competitive or account audit was completed when access failed.
