# Compliance and platform safety

The goal is clients without getting banned, blocked or sued, and without misleading anyone. When unsure, choose the stricter option.

## Always
- **Drafts only.** The founders send, post, apply and connect. You never click send, post, like, follow, connect, apply or join.
- **Disclose affiliation** whenever Eravue is mentioned or linked.
- **No deception:** no fake "happy customer" recommendations, burner or sockpuppet accounts, vote manipulation, or pretending to be neutral.
- **Opt-outs are permanent:** `--status do_not_contact`.
- **Public business contact data only:** don't scrape private profiles, bypass logins or CAPTCHAs, or evade rate limits. If a site blocks access, stop and tell the user.

## X, LinkedIn, Instagram
- Write each reply and DM individually, one at a time, at a normal human pace. No bulk automation tools: LinkedIn and Instagram ban accounts for automated DMs, connects and scraping.
- **LinkedIn:** connection notes without a pitch. A few dozen requests a day at most; keep the acceptance rate healthy.
- **Instagram:** DMs from a real account with posts. First DM plus at most 2 follow-ups, then stop. Founder-approved messages may be sent through the founders' own Chrome via Chrome DevTools: Instagram by **eravue-instagram** under its ramp caps, and Reddit, X, LinkedIn, email and Facebook by **eravue-outreach** under the caps in its SKILL.md §Sending approved messages. Only text a founder approved in chat, one at a time, with stop rules.

## WhatsApp
- **One-to-one only**, to numbers that businesses list publicly for enquiries. Never bulk-message, broadcast to cold contacts or add people to groups. WhatsApp bans numbers that get reported or blocked.
- At most 1 follow-up if there's no reply, then stop.
- **Mystery-shop enquiries:**
  - Keep them simple and short; don't waste the business's time with fake complex requests.
  - **Always disclose** in the next message that it was a test from Eravue.
  - 10-15 a day at most.

## Calls (India)
Keep cold calls few, personal and polite. High-volume promotional calling or SMS from a personal 10-digit number can get the number flagged or blocked under TRAI's unsolicited-commercial-communication rules. Scale by email and DMs instead.

## Cold email
- **Sending:** from a separate domain, never the main eravue.com (protects eravue.com's reputation). Low volume, verified emails, no attachments in the first email, a plain-text feel.
- **US (CAN-SPAM):** truthful From and subject lines, the sender's postal address, a clear opt-out honoured within 10 business days.
- **UK (PECR/UK GDPR):** B2B email to corporate addresses (Ltd, PLC) is allowed with sender identity plus an opt-out. Sole traders and partnerships are treated as individuals and need prior consent, so skip them or contact them via their public enquiry form instead.
- **EU (GDPR):** B2B legitimate interest. Keep it relevant to their role, include an opt-out, and keep data minimal.
- **Australia (Spam Act):** consent may be *inferred* when an email is conspicuously published and the message relates to their business role. It must identify the sender and include an unsubscribe.
- **India (DPDP Act):** the main obligations phase in through May 2027. Use only publicly listed business contact details, keep messages relevant to their business, and honour opt-outs.

## Marketplaces (Upwork, Contra, Fiverr)
- Keep communication and payment on-platform until a contract exists; taking clients off-platform early violates Upwork's terms.
- Don't fake a portfolio or reviews. Don't do large unpaid "test" work.

## Never pitch
People in crisis, grief, medical, legal or financial distress, minors, harassment victims, or anyone who has said "no".
