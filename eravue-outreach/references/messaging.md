# Messaging

Every message must pass three checks:
1. **Specific:** it mentions something only true of this person or business.
2. **Short:** it's readable in about 10 seconds.
3. **Human:** it sounds like Sanchit or Sanchita typing, not a template or an AI.

## Voice
- Plain, warm, direct. Short sentences. Lowercase-casual is fine on X, Reddit and Instagram; normal case for email and LinkedIn.
- India WhatsApp and Instagram: friendly and respectful ("Hi Neetu ji" only if that fits the person), with light Hinglish if the prospect writes that way. Don't force it.
- **Banned** (these read as spam or AI):
  - "I hope this message finds you well", "I came across your profile and was impressed"
  - "leverage", "synergy", "revolutionize", "game-changer", "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "cutting-edge"
  - "Dear Sir/Madam", "we are a leading company"
  - emoji walls, long em-dash chains, numbered feature lists in a first message
- Never claim results Eravue doesn't have (see [eravue-profile.md](eravue-profile.md)). "We're a small team, you'd work directly with the founders" is true and good.
- **Disclose affiliation** whenever Eravue is mentioned: "I run Eravue, an AI/web studio" or "my agency does this".
- One message, one ask. The ask is small: "want me to send it?", "worth a quick look?"

## First-message structure (all channels)
1. **Hook:** the specific thing you noticed (their post, their gap, their recent work).
2. **Value:** one useful idea, finding or free sample.
3. **Ask:** a low-pressure next step.
4. **Who:** one line on who you are, plus at most one proof link.

## Templates (adapt every time; never send unchanged)

**Reddit/X public reply to an advice thread** (only where rules allow):
> For [their exact problem], the thing that usually fixes it is [concrete tip], then [second tip]. If you're on [their tool], [specific setting/workflow].
> (Disclosure: I run a small studio that builds these, so I'm biased. Happy to share the setup if useful.)

No link unless the rules clearly allow it. It must still be useful if the last line is deleted.

**Reply/DM to a hiring post:**
> Hey [name], saw you're looking for [exact thing] for [their project]. We do exactly this at Eravue. Recent: [one relevant proof link]. Quick idea for yours: [one specific suggestion]. Happy to [do a short sample / jump on a call]. What's your timeline?

**Instagram DM (outbound):** for real Instagram outreach use the eravue-instagram skill's dm-playbook.md (shorter openers, no links, research-backed). This longer version suits email or a reply after they've answered:
> Hi [name]! Love [specific post/product]. Noticed [gap, e.g. a lot of people asking price in the comments on your last 3 reels]. We set up instant replies that answer those and book the visit for you. I run Eravue (we did Emson Designs' site). Want me to send a 1-min video of how it'd look for [business]?

**WhatsApp (outbound, after a mystery-shop test):**
> Hi [name], Sanchit here from Eravue. Full disclosure: the enquiry I sent on [day] about [x] was a test. It took [time] to get a reply. Most people message 2-3 places and go with whoever answers first. We set up instant WhatsApp replies that answer, qualify and book automatically. Can I show you a 2-min demo made for [business]? If not relevant, no worries at all.

**Cold email (international). Subject:** lowercase and specific, e.g. `missed calls at [business]`, `quick idea for [business]'s booking`
> Hi [first name],
>

>

**Follow-ups** (same thread; each adds something new; never "just bumping this"):
- **#2 (day 3):** a new angle or proof: "Made a quick mockup of what this looks like for you: [link]".
- **#3 (day 7):** a useful tip they can use without you.
- **#4 (day 14), breakup:** "Assuming now's not the time. I'll leave it here. If [problem] comes up later, I'm one message away."

**LinkedIn connection note** (300 characters max, no pitch):
> Hi [name], saw your post on [topic]; [one genuine reaction]. I build AI and automation for [their industry] businesses at Eravue. Would be good to connect.

**Upwork proposal:**
> [Line 1 restates their exact goal in their words, plus your approach in one sentence.]
> How I'd do it:
> 1. [step specific to their stack]
> 2. [step]
> 3. [step]
> Relevant: [1-2 proof links]. [A question that shows you read the brief, e.g. "Is the lead data coming from Meta lead forms or your website?"]
> I can start [today/tomorrow]. Sanchit, Eravue

Keep it under 150 words. No "Dear Hiring Manager". Answer any screening questions directly.

**Partner pitch (agency white-label):**
> Hi [name], your team clearly knows [their strength, e.g. Meta ads for real estate]. A lot of agencies tell us clients now ask for WhatsApp bots / AI follow-up / websites and they don't have the devs. We build those white-label: your brand, your client, we just deliver. Open to a 15-min chat to see if it fits?

**Warm intro request (network):**
> Hey [name]! Sanchita and I have started Eravue. We set up AI that replies to customers instantly, websites, reels and so on. Not selling you anything 🙂 but do you know any business owner who's drowning in WhatsApp enquiries or calls? An intro would mean a lot.

## Loom / video script (60-90 seconds)
1. **(0-10s)** "Hi [name], I'm Sanchit from Eravue. Made this just for [business]." Their website or Instagram is on screen.
2. **(10-40s)** The gap, shown on screen: the slow reply timestamp, the PageSpeed score, the unanswered comments.
3. **(40-70s)** The fix: a mock of their own enquiry being answered instantly, or a mockup of their new homepage.
4. **(70-90s)** "If you want this live in a week, reply here. If not, hope the tip helps."

No AI avatars. A real face and voice convert better.

## Objection replies (short, honest)
- **"Too expensive":** "Totally fair. What's the budget? We can start with just the piece that saves the most time, and add more once it pays for itself."
- **"We already reply fast":** "Love that. Most don't. Want to see how it handles 11pm and Sundays? That's usually where enquiries slip."
- **"Meta/WhatsApp has a free AI now":** "It's a good start, and we can set it up for you. Where we help is connecting it to your calendar, CRM and follow-ups so leads don't just get a reply, they get booked."
- **"Send details":** reply with a 3-line summary plus one proof link, then ask one question that moves toward a call.
- **"Not now":** "No problem. Mind if I check back in [month]?" Then set `--next-due`.
- **"Not interested" / "stop":** thank them and set `--status do_not_contact`. Never contact them again.
