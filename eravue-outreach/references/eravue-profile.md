### Positioning notes
- **Sell outcomes, not "a chatbot".** Meta launched a free Business AI on WhatsApp for Indian small businesses in 2026, and Jio Haptik sells WhatsApp AI agents from about ₹10k. A plain bot is becoming a commodity. Eravue sells the whole system: instant reply → qualification → CRM → follow-ups → booking, connected to the client's own tools.
- **Low-cost foot in the door:** "We'll set up Meta's free WhatsApp Business AI properly for you" (small fixed fee). Then upsell lead follow-up and CRM.
- **Speed is the edge:** websites in days, first automation live in a week.
- **Values to reflect in tone:** plain language, honest timelines, fixed quotes. "Sometimes the right answer is you don't need AI for this."
