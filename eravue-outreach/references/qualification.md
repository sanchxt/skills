# Qualification

Score every lead before adding it. Use the single 1-5 `--score` below, and put the reasoning in `--signal` and `--notes`.

## Score (1-5)

| Score | Inbound (they asked) | Outbound (you found a gap) |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | Explicitly hiring or asking for a provider for something Eravue does. Fresh (Upwork/X under 72h, others under 7d). Reachable. | Strong fit niche. Clear, evidenced gap Eravue fixes fast. Spending or hiring signal. Direct contact surface. |
| **4** | Clear pain plus asking for advice or tools. Fresh. A helpful reply could lead to work. | Good fit and a clear gap, but no spending or urgency signal. |
| **3** | Adjacent pain. Or a hiring post that is older (under 30d) or crowded (many replies). | Fit is plausible, but the gap is weak or the contact path is poor. |
| **2** | Weak or vague. Research value only. | Poor fit or no visible gap. |
| **1** | Off-topic, joke, spam. | Wrong market or no way to help. |

Track scores 3-5. Draft for 4-5 first. Give score 3 a lighter-touch message or keep it for slow days. Don't track 1-2; if the user asked for research, list them separately as market research.

**Boost** a score when:
- a budget is stated (any amount; there's no minimum)
- they've spent money before (Upwork history, running ads)
- the business is owner-led
- a past-client niche fits (interior design, astrology/consulting, bakery/food, training)
- you can deliver a free sample in under 20 minutes

**Lower** a score when:
- there are 30+ proposals or replies already
- the "client" has a history of scams or unpaid test work
- the buyer is enterprise procurement (slow), or the scope is clearly beyond a two-person team

## Fit label (put it in `--notes` when not obvious)
- `reply-worthy`: a public reply on the thread is appropriate.
- `dm-worthy`: the post invites DMs, or a private message is clearly welcome.
- `email-worthy`: there's a public business email or contact form and a fuller note fits.
- `proposal`: Upwork or another marketplace; a proposal is the channel.
- `research-only`: useful demand evidence, but don't contact. Don't track.
- `avoid`: sensitive, rule-prohibited, hostile to promotion, deleted or locked, or Eravue can't help. Track only if you need to stop it reappearing (`--status avoid`).

## Sensitivity (always check)
- **low:** ordinary business, creator or startup need. OK.
- **medium:** money stress, job loss, burnout. Help first, keep any pitch very light, or skip.
- **high:** crisis, medical, legal or financial distress, grief, harassment, minors. **Never pitch.** Skip.

## Quick red flags (skip)
- They want work "for exposure" or unpaid samples with large scope. A small sample offered by Eravue is fine.
- They ask to pay outside a marketplace's protection, or send odd payment links.
- Crypto/NFT pump projects, gambling, adult content, MLM.
- The post was deleted or filled ("found someone, thanks").
