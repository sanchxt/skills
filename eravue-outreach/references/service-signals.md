# Service signals: how each need shows up

For each service:
- **Ask phrases**: what people write when they're looking to hire (inbound).
- **Pain phrases**: what they write before they know a solution exists.
- **Visible gaps**: what you can see from outside (outbound).
- **Best hunting grounds** and a **first offer**.

Mix ask and pain phrases with the query operators in [inbound-channels.md](inbound-channels.md). Ask phrases are the hottest: those people already have a budget and intent.

---

## ai-chat-agents · lead-followup
- **Ask:** "whatsapp chatbot", "whatsapp automation", "auto reply instagram DMs", "chatbot for my website", "AI agent for customer support", "manychat expert", "WATI / Interakt / AiSensy setup", "need someone to set up whatsapp business api", "lead follow up automation", "speed to lead"
- **Pain:** "can't reply to all DMs", "leads go cold", "customers ask the same questions", "missing enquiries at night", "people ask price and ghost", "our team takes hours to reply"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - Bio says "DM for price".
  - Unanswered price questions in Instagram comments.
  - Only a contact form, with no WhatsApp click-to-chat.
  - Slow reply to a test enquiry: a mystery shop, disclosed afterwards.
  - Running Meta lead or click-to-WhatsApp ads with no instant response.
- **Hunting grounds:**
  - Upwork, r/forhire, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/realtors, r/AI_Agents, r/n8n (hiring posts only, check rules)
  - X: "looking for" + "whatsapp bot"
  - LinkedIn posts
  - Google Maps lists of clinics, real estate and interior designers
- **First offer:** a 1-minute speed-to-lead check, or a 3-step map of "what happens to your enquiry today vs with us". Cheap entry: set up Meta's free WhatsApp Business AI properly.

## ai-voice-agents
- **Ask:** "AI receptionist", "answering service", "voice agent", "Vapi / Retell / Bland developer", "AI cold caller", "call center automation", "missed call text back"
- **Pain:** "we miss calls when busy", "can't afford a receptionist", "calls after hours go to voicemail", "front desk overwhelmed"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - Phone-first businesses whose Google reviews say "never pick up the phone".
  - Limited call hours on Google Maps.
  - No online booking.
- **Hunting grounds:** US/UK/AU home services, dental and med-spa (cold email, Google Maps lists); Upwork "voice agent"; r/HVAC and r/smallbusiness (pain only, rarely promo-friendly); Indian clinics.
- **First offer:** a demo call: "call this number and our AI answers as your clinic". A 2-minute Loom of a mock call for their business.

## ai-automation · crm · erp-internal-tools
- **Ask:** "n8n developer", "Make.com expert", "Zapier expert", "automate google sheets", "automate invoices", "CRM setup", "Zoho/HubSpot/GoHighLevel setup", "custom CRM", "internal dashboard", "inventory software", "replace excel"
- **Pain:** "copy pasting data all day", "excel is a mess", "we track leads in sheets", "reports take hours", "data entry is killing us", "tools don't talk to each other"
- **Visible gaps (strong buying signals):**
  - **Hiring posts** for data-entry operators, telecallers, back-office or MIS executives on Naukri, LinkedIn Jobs or Indeed. They're paying salaries for work that can be automated.
  - Google Forms or Sheets visible in their funnel.
  - Job posts asking for "Excel expert".
- **Hunting grounds:** Upwork (very active for n8n, Make, Zapier, GHL), r/n8n and r/automation hiring threads (check rules), r/zapier, r/nocode, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, LinkedIn posts, Indian SME groups.
- **First offer:** "Send me the one task that eats the most hours; I'll map how it would run automatically." A free workflow map.

## website · seo
- **Ask:** "need a website", "website developer", "redesign my site", "landing page", "Shopify developer", "Webflow/Framer designer", "SEO help", "rank on google", "local SEO", "Google Business Profile"
- **Pain:** "website gets no enquiries", "site is slow", "not showing on google", "my developer disappeared", "website looks outdated"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - No website (only Instagram or Linktree).
  - Old or slow site (run PageSpeed on mobile).
  - Not mobile-friendly, no SSL, broken forms, no WhatsApp or call button.
  - Not ranking for "<service> in <city>".
  - Incomplete Google Business Profile, or few reviews.
- **Hunting grounds:** Google Maps (filter: no website), Instagram businesses with Linktree only, Upwork, r/forhire, r/smallbusiness, r/web_design (hiring posts only), r/Entrepreneur, LinkedIn.
- **First offer:** a free homepage mockup for high-value leads, or a 2-minute site teardown Loom. Price point: Eravue's "websites in days".

## apps
- **Ask:** "looking for a technical cofounder", "need an MVP", "app developer", "build an app like", "AI feature for my product", "agency to build my SaaS"
- **Pain:** "no-code tool hit its limits", "my dev quit", "need to launch fast"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - Just raised a seed or pre-seed round (Inc42 and Entrackr funding news, X announcements) with no product live yet.
  - A non-tech company hiring "full stack developer" for a one-off build.
- **Hunting grounds:** r/startups, r/SaaS, r/cofounder, r/indianstartups (check rules), Indie Hackers, X "looking for a dev", Upwork, Wellfound contract roles, LinkedIn founder posts.
- **First offer:** a 1-page scope and timeline. Point to Note Me Please, MyContent and Daysfold as proof.

## brand-design
- **Ask:** "need a logo", "brand identity", "rebrand", "UI/UX designer", "Figma designer", "app design"
- **Visible gaps:** clip-art logos, inconsistent visuals, a new business launching soon.
- **Hunting grounds:** r/forhire, r/DesignJobs (check format rules), Upwork, Contra, X, new D2C launches on Instagram.
- **First offer:** 2-3 quick direction moodboards. Only for score-5 leads.

## social-media
- **Ask:** "social media manager", "need someone to handle our instagram", "content calendar", "SMM agency", "instagram growth"
- **Pain:** "no time to post", "posting but no growth", "reels get no views"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - Last post more than 30 days ago, or irregular posting.
  - No Reels.
  - Low-quality creatives.
  - Strong business (good Google reviews) with a weak Instagram.
- **Hunting grounds:** Instagram (local businesses, D2C), LinkedIn posts, r/forhire, r/socialmedia (pain only), r/smallbusiness, Upwork.
- **First offer:** 3 post ideas or 1 reel concept written for *their* business, with a hook and outline.

## video-editing
- **Ask:** "looking for a video editor", "need a youtube editor", "reels editor", "shorts editor", "podcast clips editor", "editor for my channel", "paid editing gig"
- **Pain:** "editing takes me 10 hours", "can't keep up with uploads", "burning out editing"
- **Visible gaps:**
  - Creators, coaches and podcasters posting raw talking-head videos with no captions or cuts.
  - Long gaps between YouTube uploads.
  - Podcasts with no short clips.
- **Hunting grounds:** r/forhire, r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/youtubers (check rules: many allow only "hiring" flairs or have weekly threads), X ("looking for a video editor", very common), LinkedIn creator posts, Upwork, YouTube creators with 5k-200k subscribers.
- **First offer:** a free 20-30 second sample edit from one of their existing videos. It converts strongly; save it for score 4-5.

## ai-content
- **Ask:** "UGC creator", "ad creatives", "AI product photos", "AI avatar video", "need more ad variations", "creative strategist"
- **Visible gaps:** D2C brands running Meta ads (check the **Meta Ad Library**) with only 1-3 creatives or months-old ads. Product photos on plain or poor backgrounds.
- **Hunting grounds:** Meta Ad Library (search by category or competitor), Instagram D2C brands, Shopify stores, r/ecommerce, r/shopify, r/FacebookAds (pain only), Upwork.
- **First offer:** 2-3 sample AI creatives for one of their products (MyContent is proof Eravue builds this tech).

## Cross-service buying signals (any service)
- **Hiring signal:** a job post for a role Eravue can replace or supplement (editor, social media executive, telecaller, data entry, web developer).
- **Funding or launch signal:** just raised, just launched, opening a new branch, rebranding.
- **Spending signal:** running ads (Meta Ad Library, Google ads showing for their keyword). They have a marketing budget.
- **Frustration signal:** public complaints about a current vendor, freelancer or tool ("my developer ghosted me").
- **Event signal:** festive season (Diwali, wedding season), sale periods, new financial year (April in India). Businesses want campaigns and automation before peaks.
