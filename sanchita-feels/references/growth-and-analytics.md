# Growth and analytics system

Use for launch plans, posting experiments, performance reviews, and revisions.

## Growth premise

The controllable goal is a repeatable system that increases the odds of reach and loyalty. Do not promise viral results or treat follower growth as the only measure. At zero followers, prioritize rapid learning and a clear account promise; after signals accumulate, double down on repeatable winners without becoming a clone of one lucky post.

## First 30 days

When asked for a launch plan, build three phases:

- **Days 1–7: establish the promise.** Publish across 4–6 pillars and several formats. Pin three representative pieces. Do not judge the niche from one upload.
- **Days 8–21: controlled tests.** Repeat promising topics with a different hook or format. Use Instagram Trial Reels if available to test with non-followers; Meta says results can be evaluated before wider sharing and optional automatic follower sharing can occur based on early performance: <https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/>.
- **Days 22–30: build series.** Name one or two repeatable formats, create sequels from high-retention moments and comment questions, and update bio/pins if the audience's strongest response differs from the initial hypothesis.

Four daily posts are acceptable only if originality and clarity remain high. Use a 1-anchor/3-light system from the content engine. If fatigue or quality loss appears, protect two excellent daily posts before pursuing four.

## Metrics by objective

Evaluate like with like: compare similar length, format, topic, and age of post.

- **Scroll stop/appeal:** viewed vs. swiped away when available, first seconds retention, Instagram initial reach, YouTube Shorts chose-to-view signals.
- **Hold:** average view duration, average percentage viewed, completion, retention curve, rewatch/spikes.
- **Satisfaction:** shares, saves, likes/dislikes where available, comments with substance, survey signals available to the platform.
- **Conversion:** profile visits per reach, follows/subscribers per viewer, related-video clicks.
- **Community quality:** repeat commenters, useful questions, DMs that do not require individual counseling, and language showing viewers felt accurately understood.

YouTube describes three broad performance buckets—appeal, engagement, and satisfaction—and recommends using retention to identify strong or weak moments: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650> and <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415>.

Do not impose universal benchmark percentages. Establish the account's own rolling baseline once enough comparable posts exist.

## Performance diagnosis

Use this order:

- Low initial hold → revise first frame, first sentence, topic specificity, or packaging-to-content match.
- Strong start, sharp early drop → remove setup; deliver proof or recognition earlier.
- Good completion, low shares/saves → add a sharper reframe, practical sentence, or sendable audience target.
- Good engagement, low follows → clarify the account promise and use an earned follow CTA or series bridge.
- Comments but hostile confusion → inspect accuracy, ambiguity, and missing nuance; do not celebrate controversy automatically.
- One outlier → create two sequels that preserve the audience problem, not a shot-for-shot remake.

## Experiment ledger

For each post record:

- date/platform, topic/pillar, format, length, hook type, face/B-roll, audio, CTA, publish time;
- reach/views, first-second signals when available, average duration/percentage, completion, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, follows;
- one qualitative observation and one next test.

Change one major variable per test. Aggregate after at least several comparable posts; do not overreact to normal volatility on a new account.

## Weekly review output

Provide:

1. What happened, with normalized comparisons
2. Best evidence-backed hypotheses
3. Three patterns to keep
4. Three changes to test
5. Five next concepts tied to observed audience response
6. One production/process adjustment
7. Any accuracy, safety, or audience-trust concerns

## Cross-platform adaptation

Use the same clean 9:16 master but adapt packaging:

- remove platform watermarks;
- upload natively;
- add licensed music separately on each platform when needed;
- use Instagram caption/Story/pinned-comment community features;
- use a clear YouTube title, concise description, pinned comment, and related-video bridge when available;
- compare performance within each platform, not raw view counts across platforms.
