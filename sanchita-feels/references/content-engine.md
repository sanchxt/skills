# Content engine

Use this reference for positioning, idea generation, series, calendars, batching, and profile strategy.

## Positioning

SanchitaFeels should occupy this intersection:

> emotionally precise real-life moments + responsibly simplified psychology + calming, feminine human presence + one usable reframe

The account is not a clinic, quote aggregator, theory-fact page, or generic motivation feed. Its advantage is emotional recognition: name the tiny behavior viewers already do, explain a plausible mechanism without overclaiming, and leave them feeling seen rather than analyzed.

Useful audience jobs:

- "Put words to what I am feeling."
- "Help me understand why this interaction hurt."
- "Give me something I can send to a friend or partner."
- "Give me one gentle action I can take today."
- "Tell me whether this viral theory is a metaphor, a tradition, or an evidence-backed idea."

## Pillars and recurring series

Keep a balanced portfolio. A weekly mix can shift with performance, but do not let speculative theory content become the account's authority base.

1. **You are not weird for this** — hyper-specific behaviors after ghosting, conflict, rejection, loneliness, or a breakup.
2. **What may be happening underneath** — one carefully sourced psychological mechanism in ordinary language.
3. **The kind next move** — a boundary script, reflection prompt, grounding action, or communication option.
4. **Internet theory, honestly explained** — origin/status, why it resonates, what it can and cannot tell us.
5. **Quiet reset** — meditation, sensory grounding, peaceful nature clips, or a short permission slip.
6. **Both sides of the story** — how a behavior such as ghosting can hurt the receiver and sometimes emerge from avoidance, overwhelm, fear, or safety needs. Do not flatten abusive or unsafe situations into "both sides."
7. **A sentence you can borrow** — saveable wording for closure, boundaries, checking in, or repair.
8. **Comment-to-video** — answer a de-identified, non-diagnostic audience question and never expose private details.

## Idea score

Score candidate ideas from 0–2 on each axis. Prefer ideas scoring 8 or more out of 12, while retaining occasional low-reach trust builders.

- Recognition: can viewers identify themselves within two seconds?
- Tension: is there a clear unanswered question or contradiction?
- Usefulness: does the viewer leave with a reframe, sentence, or action?
- Sendability: is there a specific person someone would send it to?
- Visual fit: can the first frame communicate before sound?
- Distinctiveness: is this more specific than generic self-love advice?

## Four-post daily ladder

Four posts a day is a target, not a reason to mass-produce repetitive content. Build from reusable footage and research:

- **Anchor (high effort):** 25–45 seconds, face-led or strong voiceover, researched insight.
- **Relatable micro (low effort):** 7–15 seconds, one sharp observation over original B-roll.
- **Saveable tool (medium effort):** 15–30 seconds, a script, three-step reframe, or grounding prompt.
- **Conversation or trend test (low/medium):** a comment reply, theory check, current audio format, or polarizing-but-fair question.

On a two-post day, publish the anchor plus either the relatable micro or saveable tool. Batch hooks, record face-led anchors together, maintain an original B-roll bank, and turn one researched topic into distinct—not duplicated—angles.

## Format rotation

- Face-to-camera confession or observation
- Walking/nature B-roll with voiceover
- Silent aesthetic clip with text-led reveal
- Two-character self-dialogue
- "What people think / what it can feel like" contrast
- Object or visual-metaphor demonstration
- Comment screenshot reply with privacy-safe text
- Mini story: moment → meaning → next step
- Myth/metaphor/evidence check
- Guided 10–20 second grounding pause

Avoid an all-aesthetic feed. A face and recognizable voice accelerate trust; even faceless posts should carry original narration, writing, or footage.

## Launch profile

When asked to build the account from zero, create:

- a clear name field containing searchable niche language without falsely claiming a credential;
- a bio with audience + promise + tone, e.g. "putting feelings into words • relationships, healing & gentle psychology • new reels daily";
- a calm, legible profile photo with strong facial contrast or a simple original mark;
- three pinned posts: "start here," strongest emotional recognition piece, and a useful/saveable tool;
- highlights only when there is material to fill them, such as Start Here, Breakups, Boundaries, Calm, or Theories.

Do not delay publishing for a perfect visual identity. Use a small repeatable system: two text styles, one warm neutral palette, consistent subtitles, and a recognizable sign-off or series label.

## Calendar output

For calendars, include date, pillar, viewer situation, hook, format, footage source, effort level, primary engagement goal, research needed, and repurposing notes. Balance the week across:

- reach: broad but precise relatability;
- trust: sourced explanations and nuance;
- utility: scripts, prompts, and saveable tools;
- community: genuine questions and comment replies;
- identity: creator presence, story, values, and calm lifestyle footage.

Never schedule four near-identical breakup posts just because one topic is large. Repetition should build a recognizable series, not viewer fatigue.
