# Format menu

A format is the container: what the viewer sees and how the video is built. Hooks (hooks.md) and structures (retention.md) go inside it. If every video uses the same container, the account feels like a template and viewers stop noticing it. The same happens when every account uses the same container.

**Rotation rule:**
- No two videos in a batch share a format.
- Within an account, don't post the same format back to back unless the log shows it's clearly winning. When it is, make it a named series and keep varying everything else.

**The twist rule:** a plain format is a template. Each video needs one thing about the container that's unexpected: a strange constraint, a reversal of who's speaking, an unusual prop, a payoff in the visuals instead of the words. The twist is what stops it feeling AI-made.

## On camera

| Format | What it looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Talking head, one take** | Straight to camera, mid-thought, jump cuts | Takes, confessions, news breakdowns |
| **Green-screen commentary** | Creator in front of a headline, screenshot, tweet or chart, pointing at it | News, reacting to something specific, receipts |
| **Two-person scene** | Natural dialogue, interruptions, one plays the viewer | Tests, BTS, debates |
| **Skit / multi-character** | One or more people playing roles, costume or angle changes | Comedy, situations, "every X be like" |
| **POV** | The camera *is* someone, and the creator reacts to them | Relatable comedy, relationships |
| **Reply to a comment** | A comment sticker on screen, then the answer or build | Community, series, part 2s |
| **Street or friend question** | Ask real people one question, rapid cuts | Opinions, quizzes, social proof |
| **Experiment over time** | "I did X for 7/30 days", with day stamps | Habits, AI tests, transformations |

## Screen and build

| Format | What it looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Result → how-to** | The finished thing first, then 3–5 sped-up steps on screen | Tutorials, AI builds, edits |
| **Speedrun vs. a timer** | A split screen against something silly (Maggi, a match loading) | Builds, challenges |
| **Before/after transformation** | An ugly "before", a big reveal, then a detail shot | Websites, rooms, edits, businesses |
| **Live test / versus** | Two or more options tested on the same task, blind judged | AI vs. AI, AI vs. human, tools |
| **Screen-recorded story** | A chat, a DM or a notification sequence that tells the story | Relationships, clone experiments, drama |
| **Tier list / ranking** | Items placed live, with order changing as proof comes in | Tools, games, takes |

## Voice-led (no face needed)

| Format | What it looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Narration over B-roll or gameplay** | A voiceover story with matched footage | Stories, horror, psychology |
| **Multi-voice skit over gameplay** | A fake call or chat, with different AI voices | Brainrot, gamer comedy |
| **Real-clip cut** | A real moment, tightly cut, captioned | VC moments, reactions |
| **Explainer with graphics** | Voice plus simple animated text and diagrams | News, "how X actually works" |

## Text and visual-led

| Format | What it looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Text-on-screen reel** | 1–2 bold lines over calm footage, with a song | Quotable feelings, memes |
| **Text story** | 4–8 text beats over footage that tell a tiny story | Relatable moments, twists |
| **Music-led edit** | Clips cut to a drop around one angle | Character edits, montages |
| **Photo dump with a point** | Fast photos with one line that reframes them | Lifestyle, "this year", nostalgia |
| **Quiz / spot it** | "Which one is AI?", "find the mistake", with a pause-to-answer beat | Comments, replays |

## Picking well

1. Choose the format the *payoff* needs. If the payoff is visual (a transformation), show it. If it's a line, use a format that gives that line room. If it's a reaction, film the reaction.
2. Check the account's recent posts (in `log.md`) and pick something different, unless a format is a proven series.
3. Add the twist.
