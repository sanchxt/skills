## Instagram business profile checklist

- **Name field** (searchable): "Eravue | AI Automation & Websites", or with the beachhead: "Eravue | AI for Clinics & Studios".
- **Bio, 3 lines:** who it's for + outcome · proof · CTA. Example to adapt (the founders approve the final):
  > Every enquiry answered in 60 seconds, with AI agents, fast websites and reels for growing businesses 🇮🇳🌍
  > Built by @lifewithcofounders · 30+ businesses helped
  > DM "AUDIT" for a free missed-lead check ↓
- **Link:** eravue.com/contact or a WhatsApp click-to-chat with a prefilled message (use UTM `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` on site links).
- **Highlights:** Work (case studies) · Demos · Reviews · About us · Offer.
- **3 pinned posts:** best proof/case study · the founders' intro · a demo reel.
- **Contact buttons:** email, WhatsApp, category "Software company" or "Marketing agency".
- **Feed:** at least 9 recent posts before heavy DM outreach. Collab-post lifewithcofounders reels so they appear on both.

## LinkedIn personal (each founder)

- **Headline:** outcome + who + proof hook, e.g. "I build AI agents that answer every lead in 60 seconds | Co-founder @Eravue | Websites, automations & AI for growing businesses".
- **About:** first 2 lines are the hook (shown before "see more"), then who you help, what you build, proof, how to start (link + WhatsApp).
- **Featured:** case study / demo video / contact link.
- **Banner:** one line of positioning + eravue.com.
- **Experience:** Eravue role with services and project bullets. Past client work listed as projects.
- **Creator mode/settings:** turn on follow as the primary button if the option exists; check what LinkedIn currently offers.

## LinkedIn company page

Tagline matching the site, services listed, website button, logo/banner, founders linked as employees, 3 recent posts minimum.

## X

Bio: what you build + for whom + proof, link to eravue.com. Pinned: the best build thread/demo. Banner: positioning line.

## Process

1. Read each profile live (Chrome DevTools MCP, background tab, read-only).
2. Write exact replacements in `.growth/content-plan/profiles-YYYY-MM-DD.md` (current vs proposed, per field).
3. The founders approve each field. Edit via the browser only after approval, or hand the text to the founders. Changing a bio or name counts as an account-settings change, so get the explicit OK first.
4. Record the change date in strategy.md so its effect on DM reply rates can be judged.
