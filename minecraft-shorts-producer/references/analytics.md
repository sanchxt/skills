# Shorts Analytics

Use channel evidence before generic benchmarks. Ask for a channel link, analytics export, or screenshots of the best and worst Shorts when the user wants diagnosis.

## Data To Read

- title, topic, format, length, and publish date
- public views and engaged views
- shown in feed
- chose to view versus swiped away
- average view duration and average percentage viewed
- retention graph and exact drop timestamps
- likes, comments, shares, and remixes
- subscribers gained or lost
- new, returning, and regular viewers
- Shorts-feed, search, browse, and external traffic
- hook, first frame, CTA, sound, and related video

Since March 31, 2025, public Shorts views count starts and replays without a minimum watch-time requirement. Use engaged views and retention measures when judging whether viewers continued watching.

## Diagnostic Map

| Pattern | Likely bottleneck | Next test |
|---|---|---|
| Low chose-to-view | First frame or premise is weak/confusing | Replace frame zero and first sentence |
| Good chose-to-view, immediate drop | Hook and content do not connect | Continue proof immediately; remove rewind/setup |
| Gradual decline | Repetition or over-explanation | Shorten and add meaningful progression |
| Strong retention, weak engagement | Payoff lacks emotion or debate | Strengthen consequence, surprise, or decision CTA |
| Views without subscribers | No repeatable identity or future value | Name a series and promise a specific next test |
| Good metrics, limited reach | Topic demand, audience matching, satisfaction, or competition | Test an adjacent broader Minecraft noun/angle |
| Few returning viewers | Random topics or forgettable identity | Run a recognizable 3-5 video cluster |

## Comparison Rules

- Compare Shorts of similar length; average percentage viewed behaves differently at 15 and 50 seconds.
- Rank subscriber conversion using subscribers per 1,000 engaged views.
- Rank reach using shown-in-feed, chose-to-view, and engaged views together.
- Treat likes and comments as satisfaction evidence, not substitutes for viewing behavior.
- Find the exact retention drop and connect it to a spoken line, shot, or delayed payoff.
- Do not claim thresholds such as 70% chose-to-view guarantee a viral result.
- Change one major variable per follow-up so the result teaches something.

## Output

Conclude analysis with:

- do more of
- stop or fix
- next three controlled experiments
- winning hook/format pattern
- subscriber-conversion action
- data limitations
