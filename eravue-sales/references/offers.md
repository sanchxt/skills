# Offers and pricing

**Status key:** `APPROVED` may be quoted. `APPROVED (provisional)` may be quoted too (founders said the prices "seem fine" on 2026-11-11, full review pending), but list every quote you give in the daily brief so they see it. `PROPOSED` needs the founders' OK first (ask once, record the answer in `.growth/decisions.md`, update this file).

**Pricing approach (decided 2026-11-11):** no public price list on the website. Quote privately after understanding the need, but keep a few **productized entry offers** with "starting from" prices so outreach can make a concrete, low-risk ask. The founders want prices on the lower side for now, because recurring money of any size matters.

**Market context (researched 2026-11-11, vendor sources, so rough):** India WhatsApp chatbot/agency setups run about ₹25k-75k one-time plus platform fees ([hyperleap.ai](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-pricing-india-2026), [brainguru.in](https://www.brainguru.in/blog/2026/04/whatsapp-chatbot-development-cost-india/)). Monthly chatbot ops retainers run about ₹15-25k ([decipherconsultancy.in](https://decipherconsultancy.in/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-chatbot-cost-in-india-2026/)). US SMB AI receptionist tools run about $99-300/month. Advice on showing prices leans toward "starting from" for standard packages and quotes for custom work ([web60.ie](https://web60.ie/blog/show-prices-business-website-ireland), [clickx.io](https://www.clickx.io/blog/should-i-build-packages-and-show-prices-on-my-website)). Eravue's entry prices below sit deliberately under the market for the first clients.

## Custom work (always quoted)

Custom CRM, ERP/internal tools, web/mobile apps, AI products, brand identity, full SEO/GEO programmes. Offer a paid discovery or a fixed-price phase 1. Never quote a big build on the first call.

## Founding-client pilot (first 5 clients)

Up to 30% off the setup fee in exchange for: a video or written testimonial, permission to publish a case study with real numbers, and a Google review. Retainer at the normal price. Status: APPROVED (provisional).

## Floors and rules

- Floors (don't go below without the founders): the same as the starting prices above minus the pilot discount.
- Payment terms: 50% advance, 50% on delivery for setups. Retainers monthly in advance. India: UPI/bank transfer. International: **Wise** (the founders' method).
- GST: Eravue is GST-registered. India quotes say "+ 18% GST" unless the founders say otherwise (check the current rate for the service); international clients (export of services) get invoiced per the founders' accountant. Never guess tax treatment in a proposal: flag edge cases.
- If a lead states a budget, fit the scope to it rather than discounting the same scope.
