# Quotes and proposals

One page. Readable on a phone (most Indian SMB owners open it on WhatsApp). Sent as a PDF or a Google Doc link, with a 3-line WhatsApp/email summary on top.

## Template

```markdown
# [Client]: [Outcome in their words], in 7 days
Prepared by Sanchit & Sanchita, Eravue · [date] · valid 14 days

## What you told us
- [their situation + pain + their own number, e.g. "~6 enquiries a week go unanswered after 8pm"]

## What we'll set up
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
(Plain language. No tool jargon unless they asked.)

## What you'll see when it's live
- [measurable outcome they can check themselves]

## Timeline
Day 1: kickoff call (30 min) · Day 2-5: build · Day 6: you test it · Day 7: live + handover video

## Investment
- Setup: ₹X (one-time) [founding-client pilot price if approved]
- Monthly care (optional, recommended): ₹Y/month: monitoring, fixes, small changes, monthly report
- Not included: [platform/API fees paid directly by you, content you provide, etc.]

## Payment
50% to start, 50% on go-live. UPI / bank transfer. [Invoice/GST per founders' setup]

## Why us
- [1 relevant proof from the profile]
- Founders build it themselves. Fixed price, no surprises.

## Next step
Reply "go" and we'll send the advance details and book the kickoff for [date].
```

## Rules

- Prices only from offers.md entries marked APPROVED (or approved by the founders for this lead in chat).
- Scope fits the price. When budget is tight, cut scope, don't cut price.
- Always include the retainer line. Make care the default, not an extra.
- Save every proposal to `.growth/sales/<ID>-proposal.md`, update the lead to `proposal` with `--next-due` 2 days out.
- No legal contract templates beyond a simple scope + payment terms. If a client asks for an MSA/NDA, flag it to the founders.
