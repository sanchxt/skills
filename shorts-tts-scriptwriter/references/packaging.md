# Packaging

Use this for titles, descriptions, hashtags, music/sound direction, and upload notes.

## Research Before Packaging

Browse current sources when packaging depends on:

- current Roblox games, updates, codes, events, or player-count shifts
- current YouTube Shorts or TikTok meme language
- trending songs, sounds, or game-specific audio
- a specific competitor/channel style
- current monetization or policy details

Use official game pages, YouTube search, YouTube Trends surfaces when available, Roblox discovery/player-count pages, recent gaming sites, and visible competitor Shorts patterns. Prefer recent sources and state the date-sensitive basis.

## Title Rules

Create 3 title options unless the user asks for one.

Good title shapes:

- `This [Game] Trick Still Works`
- `I Got Rich in [Game] in 20 Seconds`
- `Never Do This in [Game]`
- `The Rarest [Item] I Have Ever Seen`
- `[Game] Players Are Missing This`
- `This Update Changed Everything`

Rules:

- Keep titles usually 45-70 characters.
- Put the game name or recognizable item near the front when search demand matters.
- Promise one specific payoff.
- Avoid fake codes, fake patch claims, fake giveaways, or misleading "free Robux" style bait.

## Description Rules

Use 1-3 short lines.

Template:

```text
Testing the weirdest [game] trick from the new update.
Would you risk this for [reward/item]?

#[GameTag] #Roblox #GamingShorts
```

Rules:

- Make the first line clarify the clip.
- Use a natural comment prompt.
- Keep links out unless the user specifically has a related video or product. Shorts description links are not the main growth lever.

## Hashtag Rules

Use 3-5 tags:

- 1 exact game tag
- 1 platform/community tag
- 1 format tag
- 1 trend/update tag when relevant
- 1 broad tag only if useful

Example:

`#GrowAGarden #Roblox #RobloxShorts #GamingShorts #Brainrot`

Do not stuff tags.

## Music And Sound Choice

Recommend a sound mode, not a copyrighted track unless current research finds a safe, platform-native option.

Sound modes:

- `Trending in-game or native Shorts sound`: best for Roblox brainrot, meme clips, and trend participation.
- `Fast goofy meme beat`: best for absurd wins, fails, and brainrot reveals.
- `Tense riser + impact hits`: best for steals, revenge, rare items, horror, and near fails.
- `SFX-only with low bed`: best when the voiceover and gameplay proof matter most.
- `No music, crisp SFX`: best for fast tutorial or exploit-style clips where clarity matters.

If recommending music for monetization, prefer YouTube Shorts audio, YouTube Audio Library, in-game audio with low risk, or platform-native sounds. Warn when a trend sound may limit monetization or cross-platform reuse.

## Upload Note

Include:

- best length band
- first-frame instruction
- loop instruction
- CTA type
- one A/B test variable

Example:

`Upload note: 22-28s. Start on the rare item close-up, loop back to the same close-up, test "part 2?" vs "would you risk this?" as the CTA.`
