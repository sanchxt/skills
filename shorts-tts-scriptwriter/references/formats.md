# Script Formats

Choose one format per Short. Do not blend too many ideas into one script.

## Secret Still Works

Best for Roblox, Fortnite, GTA, and update-driven games.

Structure:

1. "This trick still works..."
2. Show the requirement.
3. Show the action in two or three steps.
4. Show the reward.
5. Ask whether to test the riskier version.

## Rich From Zero

Best for Grow a Garden, tycoon games, Blox Fruits, and economy games.

Structure:

1. Show the final money/item first.
2. Reset to poor/noob state.
3. Explain the fastest path in three beats.
4. Show progress jump.
5. End with the exact result.

## Noob Mistake

Best for beginner-heavy games.

Structure:

1. Call out one painful mistake.
2. Show the wrong action.
3. Show the better action.
4. Show the difference in reward, survival, or speed.
5. Invite comments for the next mistake.

## Rare Item Flex

Best for Roblox, Minecraft, Fortnite, and games with collectibles.

Structure:

1. Show rare item immediately.
2. Ask why almost nobody gets it.
3. Show the route, odds, or hidden requirement.
4. Show final proof.
5. End on a loopable close-up.

## Betrayal Or Revenge

Best for Steal a Brainrot, GTA RP, Minecraft traps, and social games.

Structure:

1. Show someone stealing, failing, or attacking.
2. State the revenge setup.
3. Show the trap or counterplay.
4. Reveal the payoff.
5. Ask "was this deserved?"

## Update Panic

Best immediately after game updates.

Structure:

1. "The new update changed this..."
2. Show the most visually obvious change.
3. Explain what players should do first.
4. Show the new reward/problem.
5. End with "test this before it gets patched".

## Ranking Or Tier List

Best for easy batch production.

Structure:

1. State a polarizing ranking rule.
2. Reveal 3-5 items quickly.
3. Give one sentence of proof per item.
4. End with a controversial winner.
5. Ask viewers what got robbed.

## Curiosity Reveal

Best for lore, myths, glitches, and weird game details.

Structure:

1. "This looks fake, but..."
2. Show the strange thing.
3. Explain the hidden reason.
4. Show a second proof.
5. End with a quick twist or caveat.
