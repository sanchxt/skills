# TTS Style

Write for AI voice first, not silent reading.

## Voice

- Sound like a fast, amused player explaining something to a friend.
- Use simple words and clear emotion.
- Prefer "look at this", "watch this", "here's the mistake", "now it gets worse", "that actually worked".
- Avoid corporate, cinematic, or documentary phrasing.

## ElevenLabs-Friendly Punctuation

- Use short sentences.
- Use commas for tiny pauses.
- Use periods for beat changes.
- Use ellipses sparingly for suspense.
- Avoid slash-heavy text, long numbers, dense acronyms, and repeated all-caps.
- Spell out awkward symbols if needed.

Example:

Bad: "OMG THIS METHOD IS 1000000X OP!!!"

Good: "This method looks dumb. But it made me rich in twenty seconds."

## Pace

For 15-22 second Shorts:

- 45-65 spoken words.
- Hard hook in the first 8-12 words.
- One payoff.

For 23-35 second Shorts:

- 70-100 spoken words.
- Two proof beats.
- One CTA after payoff.

For 36-60 second Shorts:

- 110-160 spoken words.
- Only use this when the clip has a real story, test, or progression.

## Human Texture

Use one or two tiny imperfections per script:

- "okay, wait"
- "I thought this was fake"
- "that was way too close"
- "I should not have survived that"
- "this is actually stupid"

Do not overuse slang. One strong phrase is better than a full script of forced slang.

## CTA Style

Keep CTAs short and native:

- "Part two?"
- "Comment the next game."
- "Would you risk this?"
- "Subscribe before they patch it."
- "Tell me what to test next."

Do not ask for likes, comments, and subscribes all at once.
