# X/Twitter Lead Rubric

## Relevance Score

- `5`: Explicitly asks for a solution, alternative, recommendation, workflow help, or fix that the product directly addresses.
- `4`: Clear pain or dissatisfaction; product is a strong fit but user did not directly ask for a tool.
- `3`: Adjacent pain or market signal; useful for positioning or research.
- `2`: Weak/ambiguous fit; include only if the user asked for broad research.
- `1`: Not relevant.

## Freshness Score

- `5`: Posted within 72 hours or still actively receiving replies.
- `4`: Posted within 7 days.
- `3`: Posted within 30 days.
- `2`: Posted within 90 days.
- `1`: Older than 90 days unless viral/evergreen research.

## Engagement Score

- `5`: Strong engagement for the niche or obvious viral reach.
- `4`: Meaningful replies/likes/reposts and active discussion.
- `3`: Some engagement or from a high-signal account.
- `2`: Low engagement but specific pain.
- `1`: Little signal and little reach.

## Outreach Fit

- `reply-worthy`: Recent, relevant, non-sensitive, and a helpful reply can add value without feeling spammy.
- `research-only`: Useful for content, positioning, competitive intelligence, or market learning, but not a good direct-reply target.
- `avoid`: Sensitive, hostile, political, tragic, protected, spammy, scam-like, or a poor product fit.

## Sensitivity Risk

- `low`: Ordinary workflow, software, business, creator, hobby, or productivity problem.
- `medium`: Money stress, job loss, interpersonal conflict, identity-sensitive context, or public pile-on.
- `high`: Medical, legal, mental-health crisis, self-harm, bereavement, minors, harassment, doxxing, or highly vulnerable situations.

## Exclusion Criteria

Mark as `avoid` when:

- The user is in crisis or seeking urgent professional help.
- The post is mainly political, harassment, tragedy, adult content, or ragebait.
- The author is clearly asking not to receive promotions.
- The product mention would require deception, impersonation, or hiding affiliation.
- The thread is mostly spam, engagement farming, or bot activity.

## Output Standards

For each lead, include enough evidence that the user can decide quickly:

- What pain is visible.
- Why the product might help.
- Whether the opportunity is timely.
- How risky direct outreach would be.
- What a helpful, transparent first reply could say.
