# Channel profile

Confirmed with the owner on 2026-09-05:

- Brand: DailyGoatGame. New Instagram and YouTube accounts; zero followers at briefing, not a permanently current count.
- English. PC and console games broadly; no mobile-game coverage. Minecraft and cross-platform games are not excluded just because a mobile version exists; focus on their PC/console experience.
- Faceless owner, no owner voice. Prefer authentic streamer/game audio and caption/text edits. ElevenLabs is a last option except when directly requested.
- Mixture of reliable news, funny gameplay, and reactions. Memes occasionally; do not default to a meme-heavy account.
- Able to produce 2–3 distinct videos per day, cross-posted to both platforms. This means 2–3 videos, not 4–6 separate productions.
- Goals: views that convert to followers, comments, likes, shares, saves, more account viewing, and a returning community. Monetization later, including possible sponsors and affiliates.
- User wants a single skill entrypoint for the account, including research, specific-game ideas, scripts, and recent streamer moments. No existing competitors supplied.
- Budget and operating country deferred by owner. Do not make them prerequisites for ordinary creative work. Ask operating country when actual eligibility, contracts, taxes, or location-specific rules depend on it.

Still unknown: editor/tool access, exact daily editing time, own-gameplay capture access, existing rights arrangements, precise age distribution, profanity/spoiler preferences, and account handles/URLs. Ask only if material. For an unsolicited example, offer a clean-caption version and flag a spoiler if relevant rather than inventing a global prohibition.

## Provisional launch recommendations, not owner-confirmed rules

- Positioning: **The gaming moments worth seeing—and the updates worth knowing.** A recognizable editorial promise across games.
- Audience hypothesis: English-speaking PC/console players; US as first research lens with UK/Canada relevant secondary audiences, and other English viewers welcome. Test an 18–34 editorial lens without claiming this is the actual audience or excluding older players. Organic geographic delivery cannot be guaranteed.
- Starting content allocation across 20 posts: 8 funny/reaction moments, 6 useful visual news/update posts, 4 details/comparisons, 2 experiments (occasionally memes). Shift with observed conversion, retention, shareability, and effort.
- Keep 2 recurring game/topic clusters plus 1 rotating slot during early testing. This is an experiment in audience overlap, not a rule that multi-game channels cannot grow.
- Typical lengths: moments 12–25 seconds, suspense 18–35, visual news 15–30, details/comparisons 20–40. Usually stay within 45 seconds at launch; go to 60 when needed, and longer if a user-requested story earns it. These are editorial targets, not platform maxima.
- Two strong posts daily, third when ready and worthwhile. Do not fill a slot with a weak or unverifiable repost.

Never present these initial recommendations as a proven winning formula. The growth reference explains how to test them.
