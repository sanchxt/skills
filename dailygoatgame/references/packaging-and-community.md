# Packaging and community

## Shared master, separate packaging

Use the same substantive edit on both platforms as the owner requests. Export from the editor, not by downloading a posted Reel/Short with platform UI. The master must have audio rights suitable for both; if using platform-licensed music, apply it natively to each platform where its terms permit, which may require separate audio versions.

For a full package provide:

- First-frame text and cover wording: usually 3–6 meaningful words; make the game/scene identifiable. Do not hide the payoff object behind branding.
- YouTube title: specific game/person and actual event or question, readable without hashtags. Do not keyword-stuff. The title complements an in-feed hook; it cannot repair an unclear opening.
- YouTube description: concise context, source credit/link, relevant edition/date, correction/uncertainty if necessary. Suggest a related existing video only if it exists and is relevant; verify feature requirements if guiding setup.
- Instagram caption: short natural context plus one discussion or share/save reason; include source credit. Offer an optional Story poll or question when it helps conversation.
- Use a small set of directly relevant hashtags if useful; do not invent a magic count or guaranteed reach. Verify current input limits when producing ready-to-post batches. No irrelevant #USA targeting or #viral padding.
- One suggested pinned comment where useful. Do not post it without authorization.

News packaging should include the actual calendar date, edition/platform, and source. "Today" ages badly. A resurfaced moment should not be packaged as a new stream. A rumor label belongs in the visible video/claim, not only in a buried description.

## Convert the viewer's reason for watching

Choose one main job per post:

| Viewer value | Natural next action |
|---|---|
| Recognizable squad behavior | Send to the teammate it resembles |
| A deadline or useful setting | Save for the next play session |
| A meaningful tradeoff | Ask which option and why |
| An overlooked detail | Ask a specific interpretation backed by evidence |
| Ongoing update series | Offer future useful coverage after today's payoff |

Do not ask for likes, follows, comments, shares, and saves in one video. No-action endings often serve comedy best. Use optional prompts, not formulaic begging. Avoid rage bait, fake factual mistakes, irrelevant giveaways, and requiring a comment to access promised information.

## Brand and return visits

Proposed profile promise: **Gaming moments worth seeing. Updates worth knowing.** This is a suggested line, not an approved identity system.

For setup work, keep handle/display name consistent, make a small-size legible icon, and use repeatable series labels. Pin a representative moment, a useful update, and a clear introduction once those posts exist. On Instagram, suggest Highlights such as Updates, Best Clips, and Submit only when content for them exists. Do not fabricate testimonials or follower milestones.

Give series their own promise: "Before You Log In" for useful changes, "Look Again" for visual evidence, "Squad Moments" for recognizable comedy. Names are optional recommendations; don't impose them on every answer or present them as trademark-cleared.

For comments, answer genuine questions, acknowledge useful corrections, and invite a reason rather than a binary fight. Turn recurring questions into a new fully answered post. Keep the owner’s replies distinguishable from streamer quotes. Do not argue with every negative commenter. Draft moderation actions when needed; don't delete comments or block accounts without authorization.

Use Stories to ask what people play, test a choice, or share one update, when there is an audience to respond. Do not claim Stories reliably drive cold discovery. A small recurring exchange is more useful than inventing activity on an empty account.

## Clip submissions

When requested, draft a clear request identifying the exact clip, planned edit, YouTube/Instagram use, credit, and whether commercial use is intended. Record the owner's actual reply and the permitted scope. Never infer permission from silence, a like, or credit. Do not copy another outlet's broad legal terms as DailyGoatGame's agreement.

A submission log can track creator, original URL, source date, local file, game, requested/received permission, scope, credit, music status, edit, and published URLs. Keep private contact information out of public deliverables.
