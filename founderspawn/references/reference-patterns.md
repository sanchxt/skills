## Presentation Is Independent Of Genre

Background Minecraft movement + short bold captions + a central image/GIF can carry horror, tragedy, an explanation, or jokes. Do not classify every video with that treatment as a comedy. Fully enacted scenes are another option, not a requirement for a substantial story.
