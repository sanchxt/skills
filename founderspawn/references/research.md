# Research And Verification

Browse before relying on information that can change. Use current evidence to choose a premise; do not paste research language into the voiceover.

## Browse For

- Minecraft releases, game drops, snapshots, previews, mechanics, commands, rarity, edition differences, and version history;
- current YouTube Shorts analytics definitions, features, monetization, and policies;
- current memes, sounds, Minecraft topics, creator activity, and observable format patterns;
- rights or license conditions for music, GIFs, clips, mods, maps, and other external assets.

## Source Priority

1. Official Minecraft/Mojang release notes, help, usage guidelines, and verified announcements.
2. Official YouTube Help, Creator Insider, YouTube Creators, and YouTube Blog.
3. Official ElevenLabs documentation for TTS behavior.
4. Minecraft Wiki when primary documentation is insufficient; verify edition and version.
5. Direct creator channels, videos, and interviews for observable creative patterns.
6. Reddit and creator communities for hypotheses, complaints, and experiments—not algorithm facts.
7. Third-party channel trackers only for approximate public activity; label their numbers as third-party estimates.

## Trend And Competitor Scan

When the request depends on what works now:

1. Record the research date.
2. Inspect recent uploads and top performers separately; old hits do not prove a format is still growing.
3. Sample multiple channels and at least three distinct formats.
4. Note the first-frame promise, hook wording, duration, progression, proof/payoff, CTA, series identity, and visible authorship.
5. Separate observation from inference. Public views cannot reveal private retention or prove causation.
6. Search audience criticism and creator postmortems for saturation, trust, rights, or monetization problems.
7. Adapt the underlying viewer promise; never copy scripts, branding, recurring characters, thumbnails, or staged events.

For current meme or sound use, prefer signals repeated across more than one current source. If freshness cannot be verified, use an evergreen Minecraft-specific joke instead.

## Evidence Rules

- Cite date-sensitive recommendations and distinguish verified platform facts from creator anecdotes.
- State Java/Bedrock and version when behavior differs.
- Turn an uncertain mechanic into an honest experiment rather than an assertion.
- Do not fabricate patches, probabilities, records, seeds, leaks, trend status, or "before it gets patched" deadlines.
- Do not call copyrighted audio or a reaction clip monetization-safe without a rights basis.
- If public channel pages are inaccessible, report the limitation and avoid inventing recent uploads.
