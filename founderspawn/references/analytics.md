# Shorts Analytics

Use channel evidence before generic creator anecdotes. When diagnosis is requested, ask for or inspect the smallest useful evidence set: the best and worst comparable Shorts, their scripts, and their retention/engagement screens.

## Data To Read

- topic, format, length, publish date, and exact first frame;
- first spoken sentence and first caption;
- public views and engaged views;
- shown in feed and stayed to watch;
- average view duration and average percentage viewed;
- retention graph and exact dip/spike timestamps;
- likes, comments, shares, remixes, and subscribers gained/lost;
- new, casual, regular, and returning viewers where available;
- Shorts-feed, search, browse, and external traffic;
- spoken CTA, loop, and attached Related Video.

Since March 31, 2025, public Shorts views count starts and replays without a minimum watch-time requirement. Use engaged views and retention measures when judging whether viewers continued watching.

## Diagnostic Map

| Pattern | Likely bottleneck | Next controlled test |
|---|---|---|
| Low stayed-to-watch | Frame zero or premise is weak, familiar, or confusing | Change the visible outcome and first sentence together |
| Good feed stop, sharp 1–3s dip | Hook promises one video while the next line starts another | Continue proof immediately; remove setup or rewind |
| Mid-video cliff | A sentence repeats stakes, adds context too late, or pauses progress | Cut/reorder that exact beat; add proof or consequence |
| Smooth gradual decline | Progression is too predictable or wordy | Shorten and add a real turn, narrowing clue, or comparison |
| Spike | Rewatchable payoff, confusing explanation, or shareable moment | Identify which; expand only if it was genuinely valued |
| Drop at CTA | Ask is too long, too early, stacked, or unrelated | Move after payoff, reduce to one short native action, or remove |
| Strong retention, weak comments | Format gives no decision or identity signal | Add a bounded choice or defensible verdict |
| Views without subscribers | Video is satisfying but channel promise is unclear | Name the repeatable series value and attach a direct follow-up |
| Few returning viewers | Topics and formats feel interchangeable | Run a coherent 3–5 video cluster with recurring identity |
| Good metrics, limited reach | Topic demand, competition, seasonality, or audience matching | Test an adjacent broader Minecraft noun or emotional angle |

## Comparison Rules

- Compare Shorts of similar duration and format.
- Prefer medians across a small cluster over one viral outlier.
- Rank subscriber conversion using subscribers per 1,000 engaged views.
- Judge reach with shown-in-feed, stayed-to-watch, and engaged views together.
- Treat likes and comments as satisfaction evidence, not substitutes for watch behavior.
- Connect every retention dip to the exact spoken line, visual, or CTA at that timestamp.
- Change one major variable in a follow-up whenever possible.
- Do not repeat anecdotal thresholds as algorithm rules.

## Output

Conclude with:

- do more of;
- stop or fix;
- exact hook or beat causing the biggest leak;
- next three controlled experiments;
- subscriber/return-viewer action;
- data limitations.
