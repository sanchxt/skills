# Solo Creative + Flashback production

## User's standing workflow

The user records alone, personally performing actions in Minecraft Creative mode and using Flashback to obtain clips afterward. Apply this by default to FounderSpawn storytelling and recording plans. Preserve this workflow unless the user requests a different method.

Write ambitious stories with simple footage. Default to one controllable player, small reusable sets, static props, and separate takes joined in the video editor. Do not require another account, actor, server, NPC mod, custom animation, complex commands, elaborate redstone, or reliable mob behavior. Offer such techniques only as explicitly optional enhancements with a simple fallback.

## Required recording guidance

For every storytelling line or contiguous narration section, provide a clip ID, exact phrase range, required set/props, what the user does while recording, camera/framing to obtain in Flashback, approximate usable length, and any edit, reset, or alternate take needed. Cover the entire narration without gaps. Adjacent lines can share a clip when the image remains useful. Keep this outside the copy-ready voiceover.

Separate live performance from replay camera work: the user first records walking, stopping, looking, placing, dropping, or opening; afterward Flashback provides views of that recorded action. Replay camera movement does not create a second actor or new world events. Verify version-specific menu names, shortcuts, commands, and mod features before prescribing them; otherwise give version-neutral shot intent.

For impossible events, explain the illusion concretely: record state A, manually change the set off-camera, record state B, then cut between matching views. Specify the object positions and framing that must match. Do not describe a prop as moving by itself unless an actual executable method or edit is supplied.

For multiple characters, use off-screen dialogue, separately recorded reverse angles with a distinct held item or clothing, or static silhouettes/armor stands explicitly treated as props. Do not assume Flashback merges separate performances into simultaneous actors. Avoid necessary mob attacks, eating, sleeping, damage, hunger, or death while in Creative; imply them through narration, props, framing, and editing, or supply a verified optional alternative.

## Batch design and checks

List minimal reusable sets and props, then group recording by set while retaining individual script and clip IDs. Distinguish background gameplay from literal reenactment. Explicitly mark fictional mechanics and SCP fan-fiction additions so edited footage is not offered as evidence of vanilla behavior or source lore.

Review each plan as a solo performer: can one player stage and record every necessary action, does the payoff have a filmable image, are resets clear, and does the story still work without advanced tools? Provide simple alternatives for difficult effects. Use source-aware narration and SCP credits through research.md. Keep durations based on actual words and plausible delivery.
