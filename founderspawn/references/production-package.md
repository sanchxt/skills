# Complete Production Package

Use this only when the user asks for a full package, recording plan, edit plan, packaging, or end-to-end Short. Keep the copy-ready narration first.

## 1. Creative Core

- working concept and series name;
- primary format and emotional engine;
- one-sentence cold-viewer promise;
- target length;
- required edition/version, mod, world setup, or story disclosure.

## 2. ElevenLabs Script

Provide one final narration block using `scripting.md` and `elevenlabs.md`. Do not include drafts or alternate hooks unless asked.

## 3. TTS Direction

State mood, speed, emphasis, and pause/slow-down point outside the script.

## 4. Recording And Edit Blueprint

| Time | Gameplay/visual | Voiceover purpose | On-screen text | Edit/SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Requirements:

- frame zero, first caption, and spoken hook express the same premise;
- captions normally use 2–6 words and stay inside the vertical safe area;
- visual changes occur when the meaning, proof, emotion, or action changes—not on an arbitrary timer;
- reaction GIFs/chips punctuate a beat and leave before they obscure the Minecraft story;
- identify every shot that must be recorded, staged, replayed, split-screened, or sourced under an appropriate license;
- state world setup, commands, seed, items, mobs, replay/mod tools, edition, and version when relevant;
- show the promised proof or result on screen;
- use a loop only when the final moment naturally reconnects to frame zero.

## 5. Upload Packaging

- three honest, curiosity-first title options, usually 35–65 characters;
- one or two description lines that clarify the clip and ask at most one question;
- three to five relevant hashtags only when useful;
- safe music mode and exact original/licensed SFX direction;
- first-frame text;
- related-video choice that continues the exact interest of this Short;
- channel-grid thumbnail suggestion only when useful outside the Shorts feed.

Do not rely on description or comment URLs as the main next-view path. When advanced feature access is available, use YouTube's Related Video field.

## 6. Growth Test

Include:

- retention reason: why the next beat must be watched;
- return reason: what recognizable value the series promises again;
- one A/B variable only;
- one primary metric and one secondary metric;
- `Data basis`: channel data, current research, verified Minecraft source, or evergreen assumption.

## Quality Check

Confirm:

- a cold viewer understands the premise by about two seconds;
- the middle progresses through causes, proof, or narrowing clues;
- the payoff resolves the opening exactly;
- the CTA follows value and asks for one relevant action;
- the concept is not an interchangeable template or shallow remix;
- factual claims and version differences are verified;
- every shot is recordable and every external asset has a plausible rights path.
