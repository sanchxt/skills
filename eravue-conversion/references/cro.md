# Conversion checklist for eravue.com

Walk each page as the three main visitors: (1) an Indian clinic/studio owner on a phone who got an Instagram DM, (2) a US home-services owner from a cold email, (3) an Upwork client checking the portfolio. Answer for each: do they understand what Eravue does for *them* in 5 seconds, do they believe it, and is the next step obvious and easy?

## Global

- [ ] One primary CTA per page, visible above the fold on mobile. India-leaning pages lead with WhatsApp; international with "Book a free call".
- [ ] WhatsApp floating button has a prefilled message naming the page/offer (so the founders know the context).
- [ ] Response promise near CTAs ("We reply within 1 hour, 9am-9pm IST").
- [ ] Real proof near CTAs: client logos, the Emson testimonial, products, the "30+ businesses helped" stat (confirmed true by the founders 2026-11-11).
- [ ] Founders' faces and names somewhere on key pages. People buy from people, especially from a new agency.
- [ ] Page speed: LCP < 2.5s on mobile (the seo skill has audit tooling).
- [ ] No dead ends: blog posts, products and portfolio all route to a relevant service and CTA.

## Contact page

- [ ] Form: as few required fields as possible (name, WhatsApp/email, what they need). Phone/WhatsApp field for Indian leads.
- [ ] Alternatives: WhatsApp, call, email, booking link if the founders adopt one (Cal.com/Calendly free tier).
- [ ] What happens next: "we'll reply within X hours with a 15-minute call slot".
- [ ] Success state tells them what happens next and offers WhatsApp for a faster reply.

## Service and landing pages

- [ ] Headline = outcome for a specific buyer, not the service name.
- [ ] Pain → how it works (3 steps) → what you get → proof → FAQ (price expectations, timeline, "do I need tech skills") → CTA.
- [ ] "Starting from" prices only for APPROVED productized offers (eravue-sales offers.md). Otherwise "fixed quote after a 15-min call".
- [ ] Demo video/GIF of the actual thing (AI agent replying, site before/after).

## Portfolio and case studies

- [ ] Each project: client, industry, problem, what was built, result (only real numbers), link, testimonial if any.
- [ ] Case studies get their own URLs (shareable in DMs and proposals).

## Prioritizing

Score each fix: impact (how many visitors × how close to the CTA) × confidence ÷ effort. Do top-down. Record what shipped and when, so later conversion changes can be read against it.
