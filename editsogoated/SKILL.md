# EditSoGoated

Hard edits: characters from movies, shows, anime, games or sport, cut to music with velocity ramps, shakes, flashes and a drop. There's no voiceover. The edit *is* the script.

**Before planning, read** `../short-form-craft/SKILL.md` (the sibling skill folder) and its `references/hooks.md` (especially result-first, contradiction, and pure visual/sound) and `references/critic.md`. Read `log.md` here and lean toward what has worked.

## The thing most edits get wrong

Pretty clips on a beat is a montage, and the feed is full of them. The edits that travel have an **angle**: one idea the cuts prove. Viewers send an edit because of the angle ("bro he really was the goat", "this scene still hurts"), not because of the transitions.

Angle patterns:
- **Contradiction:** what the character says vs. what the clips prove ("Do I really look like a guy with a plan?" over every plan working).
- **Then vs. now:** a younger self or the start of an arc, then the final form.
- **The receipt:** a criticism or doubt, then the moment that answered it.
- **One trait, every time:** every time he smiled before something terrible happened.
- **The scene that broke fans:** the build, then the moment.
- **Villain was right**, or **the side character carried**: a defensible fan argument that gets comments.
- **Duo or mirror:** two characters cut in parallel, with matching motion.

The angle should be graspable from the first frame plus at most 3–7 words of text, or from a single line of dialogue.

## Anatomy of a hard edit (about 10–25s)

| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–1.5s | The strongest frame, or a cold dialogue line on near-silence. Text only if it sharpens the angle. |
| **Build** | ~1.5–5s | Slower clips with rising audio. Flashes or cuts on hi-hats. Tension. |
| **Drop** | on the drop | The most iconic, highest-motion clips. Velocity ramps (slow at the peak of the motion, snap fast on the exit), shake, flash and zoom-punch on hits. Cut every beat or half-beat. |
| **Turn** (optional) | mid | A one-beat pause, a freeze frame or a dialogue fragment that lets the angle land. |
| **End** | last 1–2s | The final image that settles the angle, held. Hard cut, or a loop back to the hook frame. |

Match motion across cuts (a punch into a turn into a swing). Use one effect language per edit. Stacking every effect is how an edit looks like a template.

## Songs

Never call a sound trending from memory. When choosing audio, search for what's currently hot for edits on TikTok and Reels. Give 2–3 options with the reason each fits (energy, drop timing, mood), plus one evergreen fallback. Tell the user to add the sound from the platform's own library when posting, and to export the edit with that exact cut of the song in mind.

## Clips

Describe scenes precisely (who, what's happening, the episode or film) and only state episode numbers or timestamps you're sure of. Otherwise write "find: <scene description>". The edit has to be transformative (cutting, effects, the angle), which is what keeps it clear of Instagram's April 2026 repost penalty. Never post a straight unedited clip.

## Output (per edit)

```
### <character> - <angle in a few words>, ~<N>s
Angle: one sentence. Why someone sends it: one line.
Hooks (3): first frame + optional text / dialogue line ★ marked
Song: 2–3 options + fallback (verified current if called trending)
Beat map:
  0.0–1.5  HOOK   clip … | effect … | text …
  1.5–4.0  BUILD  …
  4.0      DROP   clip, clip, clip … (motion match notes)
  …        TURN / END
Text overlays: exact words (lowercase usually reads more native)
Caption: 1 line + a comment hook that invites a real argument
```

## Example that sets the bar

**Joker (The Dark Knight): "a guy with a plan", about 18s**
- **Angle:** he says he's an agent of chaos, and every clip shows a plan working. **Send line:** "he planned EVERYTHING".
- **Hook ★:** near-black, then his face in the hospital scene, with the line *"Do I really look like a guy with a plan?"* and no music.
- **Build:** the bank-heist opening, the crew getting picked off one by one. Cut on the hats.
- **Drop:** the interrogation room (he got caught on purpose), the pencil trick, the hospital walking away, Harvey's half-burned face (the turn he engineered). Velocity on every motion.
- **Turn:** freeze on the interrogation room. Text: `he got caught on purpose`.
- **End:** back to the hospital-scene line, held for one beat. Loop.
- **Caption:** `chaos with a calendar.` Comment hook: "most planned villain in movies? wrong answers only."

## Logging results

When results come in, append them to `log.md`: date, character, angle, song, length, views, % viewed / watch time, shares, and a one-line takeaway. Re-read it before suggesting new edits.
