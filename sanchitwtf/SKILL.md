# sanchitwtf

Sanchit's personal Instagram. He's a technical guy who lives on the internet and plays with every new AI tool the week it drops. He handles memes and skits himself. **This skill's job is the ideas he can't think of on a blank day:** fun AI experiments, "how I made this" tutorials on modern tools, creator tricks, and community series that turn viewers into followers.

**Before writing, read** `../short-form-craft/SKILL.md` (the sibling skill folder) plus its `references/hooks.md`, `references/human-voice.md` and `references/critic.md`. Read `log.md` here and lean toward what has worked.

## What works here

**1. AI experiments with a story.** He does something slightly unhinged with AI, and the *result* is the hook: "I let AI plan my entire Saturday", "I cloned my voice and called my mom", "I asked five AIs the same stupid question", "I gave AI my bank statement and asked it to roast me". There should be a reaction or consequence at the end, not just a demo.

**2. "How I made this" tutorials.** The finished result is on screen first, then 3–5 sped-up steps with the real tool and prompt visible, then "comment [WORD] for the prompt". Pick things people actually want to make: an AI video of themselves, a custom app, an edit effect, a profile picture, an AI ad for a small business, a song from their group chat.

**3. Speedruns and challenges.** Building something absurd against a dumb timer, like before the Maggi's ready or before the match loads. The timer is on screen and the result is judged at the end.

**4. Community series (the follower engine).** The flagship is **"I Build What You Comment"**: take the funniest or most-wanted comment, build it with AI (an app, a tool, a song, a video, a website), show it working, and end with "comment what I build next". Suggest an episode in most idea batches. Other series: "Group Chat Records" (viewers' worst messages become songs), "send me your worst photo, I'll make it a movie poster", "four AIs judge your [thing]". Name the series on screen, and end every episode with the next call for submissions.

**5. Fun takes on tech culture.** "Every AI startup demo be like", "things that are AI now that didn't need to be". Use sparingly, and only when the take is sharp.

He speaks English by default, with natural Hinglish where that's how he'd say it.

## Skip these

- **Family-judging and reaction formats:** "AI recipe vs Mom", relatives reacting to AI, "Indian parents be like". They're cringe and tired.
- **Funny skits and memes:** he makes those himself, so only pitch them if asked.
- **Builds that help a real business** (a website for a shop, an ad for a stall): those belong to **lifewithcofounders**, where they bring in clients. Here, builds are for fun, for the comments, or for him.

## Output

When asked for ideas on a blank day (the most common request), give 8–10 one-liners. Each has the hook line, what's on screen in the first second, and why someone would send it or comment. Fully script the top 3 using the short-form-craft format: hooks, every line, visuals, caption, CTA. When asked for a specific number of ideas (5 or fewer), fully script all of them. Tool names and features must be real; search if unsure whether something exists or is free.

## Example that sets the bar

**Speedrun tutorial, about 35s.** Hook text: `app vs maggi`
```
[0:00, split screen: Maggi on the stove | laptop. Timer: 2:00]
SANCHIT: Maggi says two minutes. I'm building an app before it's done.
[0:03, screen recording, timer running]
SANCHIT: AI app builder. Prompt: "app that rates how burnt my food is from a photo."
[0:09] First version's broken. Obviously.
[0:12] SANCHIT: "Fix the camera button." Fixed.
[0:16, stove: steam, nobody watching it]
[0:20, timer 0:04] SANCHIT: Deploy. Deployed.
[0:23, he turns around. The Maggi is burnt.]
SANCHIT: ...
[0:26, he points the app at the Maggi. Result on screen: "2/10. who did this."]
SANCHIT: It works.
TEXT: comment MAGGI for the prompt
```
Why it works: real stakes on a timer, two fast stages of building, and the payoff is a consequence: the thing he ignored becomes the test case.
