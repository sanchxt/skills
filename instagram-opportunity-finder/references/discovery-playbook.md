# Instagram Discovery Playbook

## Prospect Cluster Prompts

Convert the user's offer into account clusters:

- Business type: local service, ecommerce, coach, consultant, creator, SaaS, agency, venue, marketplace seller, educator, community, nonprofit.
- Role: founder-led brand, solo creator, marketing manager, owner-operator, boutique team, freelancer, creator monetizing an audience.
- Need signal: unclear bio offer, outdated website, weak landing page, inconsistent content, no lead magnet, poor booking flow, underused reels, no portfolio proof, low engagement on sales posts, visible rebrand, new product launch, hiring/growth signals.
- Exclusions: minors, private accounts, crisis/vulnerable content, accounts that clearly serve a different region/language/budget, accounts with no commercial intent.

## Discovery Channels

- Instagram search: keywords, account categories, hashtags, locations, "suggested for you", public followers/following/commenters when authorized.
- Search engines: `site:instagram.com "<niche>" "<city>"`, `site:instagram.com "<keyword>" "founder"`, `site:instagram.com "<service>" "<industry>"`.
- Web ecosystems: competitors' testimonial pages, directories, Shopify/Wix/Squarespace stores, creator marketplaces, podcast guest pages, event speaker pages, local business directories.
- Adjacent platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, newsletters, Product Hunt, marketplaces, directories, and official websites that link back to Instagram.

## Search Pattern Examples

For a web development and marketing agency:

- `site:instagram.com "esthetician" "book now" "Dallas"`
- `site:instagram.com "handmade jewelry" "shop" "DM"`
- `site:instagram.com "coach" "link in bio" "waitlist"`
- `site:instagram.com "restaurant" "catering" "new menu"`
- Instagram hashtags: `#smallbusinessowner`, `#shopifystore`, `#salonowner`, `#coachlife`, `#newbrand`, `#supportlocal`.

For creator/founder outreach:

- Look for creators with recurring calls to action but weak landing pages.
- Look for founders posting product updates without clear conversion paths.
- Look for agencies or consultants whose public positioning is fragmented or stale.

## Prioritization

Prioritize accounts that meet at least three of these:

- Public and active in the last 30-60 days.
- Clear commercial intent or audience monetization.
- Visible gap the user's offer can address.
- Website or bio link has conversion/design/content issues.
- Account appears owner-operated or founder-led, making outreach plausible.
- Has a reachable surface: DM open, public email, website contact form, booking form, or recent comment activity.
- The user's proof points map cleanly to the account's likely pain.
