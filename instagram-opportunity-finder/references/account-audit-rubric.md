# Account Audit Rubric

## Audit Checklist

Capture evidence, not guesses:

- Profile: username, display name, profile URL, account type, niche, location, bio, contact buttons if visible, follower range, activity recency.
- Positioning: who they serve, what they sell, whether the offer is understandable in five seconds.
- Content: content pillars, posting consistency, format mix, sales/content balance, engagement quality, pinned posts, proof/testimonials, calls to action.
- Conversion path: bio link, link-in-bio page, website, store, booking, newsletter, lead magnet, analytics/pixel clues only when visible.
- Website: mobile polish, speed perception, design quality, copy clarity, SEO basics, forms/booking, trust proof, pricing/offer clarity, broken links.
- Outreach surface: DM, public email, website form, comment, LinkedIn/site contact, or no safe contact path.

## Scoring

Use `1`-`5` scores:

- `icp_fit_score`: 5 means the account closely matches the user's target segment, budget, and offer; 1 means mostly unrelated.
- `urgency_score`: 5 means visible recent signals suggest a current need; 1 means no time-sensitive need.
- `business_value_score`: 5 means the account likely has meaningful revenue potential; 1 means low or unclear value.

## Outreach Fit

- `dm-worthy`: Good fit, specific private suggestion, DM appears acceptable.
- `comment-worthy`: Public comment can add value without pitching; use when DM is too salesy or when the account has a relevant recent post.
- `email-worthy`: Public business email or website contact form exists and a fuller note is appropriate.
- `research-only`: Useful market insight but weak or stale for outreach.
- `not-fit`: Poor ICP fit or no credible way the user can help.
- `avoid`: Sensitive, private, underage, crisis-oriented, deceptive, restricted, or likely harmful to contact.

## Risk Rules

Mark `sensitivity_risk` as:

- `low`: ordinary business, creator, or professional account.
- `medium`: personal hardship, health-adjacent, finance-adjacent, identity/community topics, or ambiguous age/audience.
- `high`: minors, medical/legal/financial distress, mental-health crisis, grief, harassment, private or vulnerable situations.

Do not produce promotional outreach for `high` risk accounts.

## Evidence Standard

Every qualified lead needs:

- One concise evidence summary grounded in profile, content, or website observations.
- One clear gap summary, separated into content and website when possible.
- One service-fit explanation that connects the user's offer to that gap.
- At least one tailored outreach draft unless marked `research-only`, `not-fit`, or `avoid`.
