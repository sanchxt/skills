# Outreach Guidance

## General Rules

- Write like a real person who looked carefully at the account.
- Be specific, short, and useful before mentioning the user's offer.
- Do not overclaim results, shame the account, or pretend to be a customer.
- Disclose affiliation when pitching: "I run...", "I work on...", "My agency helps...", "I build..."
- Avoid mass-message sameness. Each draft should include one account-specific observation.
- Do not recommend outreach to `avoid` accounts.

## DM Pattern

Use 3-5 short lines:

1. Specific observation from their account, content, or website.
2. One useful gap or opportunity.
3. A low-pressure offer to share an idea, loom, audit, mockup, checklist, or example.
4. Optional transparent affiliation.

Example shape:

`Hey <name>, saw your recent posts around <specific thing>. Your offer is strong, but the bio link makes people work a bit to find <desired action>. I run <brand>; happy to send a quick 2-minute teardown with a cleaner flow if useful.`

## Comment Pattern

Use public comments only when they add value without pitching:

- Mention the post topic.
- Add a useful tip or observation.
- Avoid links, prices, or direct sales language unless explicitly invited.
- Move to DM/email only if the account asks.

## Email Pattern

Use email or contact forms when a public business email or website form exists:

- Subject: specific and non-clickbait.
- First line: account-specific observation.
- Body: concise audit finding plus why it matters.
- Offer: one concrete next step.
- Signature: disclose brand/account.

## Useful Outreach Offers

- 2-minute website teardown.
- One-page content/bio audit.
- Landing page wireframe.
- Reels/content calendar ideas.
- Conversion checklist.
- Before/after homepage mockup.
- Analytics/tracking checklist.
- SEO/local search quick wins.
