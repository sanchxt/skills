# Target audience file: `.seo/audience.md`

Posts rank and convert when they answer a specific person's specific question in their words. Build this once per project, refresh every 90 days or when the offer changes.

## Sources

1. **The live site** (home, services, about, pricing/contact): who it says it serves, problems it claims to fix, industries, geography, price level. The site is the current truth; old docs may describe an older business.
2. `.seo/project-profile.md` and any brand or purpose docs in the repo.
3. **Search Console queries** (via the `seo` skill): the words people already use to find the site.
4. **Communities, researched live** (Chrome DevTools MCP or WebSearch): Reddit, Quora, niche forums, LinkedIn and YouTube comments where these buyers talk. Search `site:reddit.com "<problem>"` and similar. Copy real phrasing, objections, budget talk and tools they mention.
5. **Competitor pages and reviews**: who they target and what buyers praise or complain about.

## Format

```markdown
# Audience - {project}
**Researched:** {date}  **Sources:** {list with links}

## Primary segments (2-4)
### {Segment name, e.g. "Clinic owners in Indian metros"}
- Who: {role, business type and size, location}
- Trigger moments: {what makes them search now}
- Pains in their words: "{quote}", "{quote}"
- What they've tried: {tools, DIY, agencies}
- Objections and fears: {cost, trust, complexity, data}
- Budget signals: {what they say they can spend}
- Words they use vs words we use: {e.g. "auto-reply on WhatsApp" vs "AI agent"}
- Decision questions: {what they need answered before buying}
- Best content for them: {cost guides, comparisons, how-tos, checklists, examples}
- Money page that solves it: {URL}

## Language bank
| Their phrase | Source | Use in |
|--------------|--------|--------|

## Not our audience
- {segments to avoid writing for}
```

Use the "words they use" column when choosing keywords and headings. Write for the buyer, not for other marketers.
