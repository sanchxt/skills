# Content Calendar

## Purpose

Create a strategic, data-driven content calendar with full post drafts ready for execution. Every post has a purpose, a hook, a caption, a CTA, a visual direction, and a reason for existing.

## Before Starting

**Mandatory reads — do not skip:**
1. `.growth/brand-profile.md` — Brand identity, audience, platforms, goals, content preferences
2. `.growth/competitors/` — What's working for competitors, gaps to exploit
3. `.growth/trends/{current-month}.md` — Current trends, seasonal moments, platform shifts
4. `.growth/voice-library.md` — Proven hooks, CTAs, caption styles (if exists)
5. `.growth/insights.md` — What's worked before, learnings from past content (if exists)
6. Previous month's calendar in `.growth/calendars/` — For continuity

If any critical file is missing or outdated, inform the user and offer to run that research first. A calendar without research is just guessing.

## Calendar Planning Process

### Step 1: Define the Monthly Strategy

Before creating individual posts, define the overarching strategy for the month:

- **Monthly theme**: What's the connecting thread? (can be loose — e.g., "authority building" or "community-first" or "product launch prep")
- **Key dates**: Holidays, events, launches, seasonal moments from trend research
- **Goals this month**: Specific, measurable (e.g., "increase saves by 20%", "grow followers by 500", "drive 100 link clicks")
- **Experiments**: 1-2 new things to try this month (new format, new hook style, new content pillar)

### Step 2: Content Pillar Mix

Define 3-5 content pillars with strategic purpose and percentage allocation:

| Pillar | Purpose | % of Content | Example Topics |
|--------|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Educational | Build authority, drive saves | 30% | Tips, how-tos, industry insights, myth-busting |
| Engaging | Drive comments and shares | 25% | Questions, polls, hot takes, relatable content, trends |
| Inspirational | Build emotional connection | 15% | Success stories, transformations, aspirational content |
| Promotional | Drive conversions | 15% | Product/service highlights, offers, testimonials, case studies |
| Community | Build loyalty and trust | 15% | Behind-the-scenes, UGC, collaborations, personal stories |

Adjust percentages based on the brand's current goals:
- Need more followers? → Increase Engaging and Educational
- Need more sales? → Increase Promotional (but never above 25% — too salesy kills reach)
- Need more trust? → Increase Community and Inspirational

### Step 3: Platform-Specific Format Planning

Map content types to platforms based on what performs best:

**Instagram:**
| Format | Best For | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Reels (15-60s) | Discovery, new audience reach | 2-4/week |
| Carousels (5-10 slides) | Educational, saves, shares | 2-3/week |
| Static posts | Quotes, announcements, testimonials | 1-2/week |
| Stories | Daily engagement, polls, BTS, urgency | Daily |

**X/Twitter:**
| Format | Best For | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Threads (5-10 tweets) | Deep value, thought leadership | 2-3/week |
| Single tweets | Hot takes, quotes, quick insights | 1-3/day |
| Quote tweets | Engage with trends and conversations | As relevant |
| Polls | Community engagement, market research | 1/week |

**LinkedIn:**
| Format | Best For | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Text posts (with line breaks) | Highest organic reach, stories | 3-4/week |
| Document carousels | Educational, high saves | 1-2/week |
| Polls | Engagement boost | 1/week |
| Articles/Newsletters | Thought leadership, SEO | 1-2/month |

**Facebook:**
| Format | Best For | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Video (native upload) | Highest reach | 2-3/week |
| Image posts | Community engagement | 2-3/week |
| Link posts | Traffic driving | 1-2/week |
| Group posts | Targeted engagement | As relevant |

### Step 4: Posting Schedule

Determine optimal posting times. Start with these baselines and adjust based on performance data:

**Instagram:** Tue-Thu 9-11am, Mon/Wed 7-8pm (target audience timezone)
**X/Twitter:** Mon-Fri 8-10am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm
**LinkedIn:** Tue-Thu 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm
**Facebook:** Mon-Wed 9am-12pm, Thu-Fri 1-4pm

If performance data exists in `.growth/analytics/`, use ACTUAL best times instead of these baselines.

### Step 5: Create Full Post Drafts

For EVERY post in the calendar, create a complete draft. No placeholder content. No "TBD" topics. Every post must include all of the following:

#### Post Draft Format:

```markdown
---

### {Date} — {Platform} — {Content Type}

**Pillar:** {which content pillar}
**Topic:** {specific topic}

**Hook:** "{the first line of caption / first 3 seconds — THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ELEMENT}"

**Caption:**
{Full caption text, ready to copy-paste. Include:
- Line breaks for readability
- Emojis where appropriate for the brand's tone
- Appropriate length for the platform
- Natural, conversational flow that matches brand voice}

**CTA:** {specific call-to-action — what do you want them to DO?}

**Hashtags:** {5-15 for Instagram, 3-5 for LinkedIn, 1-3 for X, 1-3 for Facebook}

**Visual Direction:**
- **Concept:** {one-line creative vision}
- **Style:** {aesthetic direction}
- **Color Palette:** {specific colors}
- **Typography:** {font treatment}
- **Composition:** {layout approach}
- **Key Visual Element:** {hero element}
- **Mood:** {emotional feel}
- **Reference Notes:** {additional direction}

**Strategic Notes:** {why this post exists — what strategic purpose does it serve?}

---
```

## Hook Writing — The Most Critical Element

Every hook must accomplish ONE thing: make someone stop scrolling.

### Hook Rules
- Stop the scroll in under 2 seconds
- Create a knowledge gap, emotional trigger, or pattern interrupt
- Be SPECIFIC — vague hooks get ignored ("5 tips for success" → BAD; "The LinkedIn post format that got me 847 leads in 30 days" → GOOD)
- Never start with "I" or "We" — start with the audience's world
- Use power words: secret, mistake, truth, myth, stop, never, always, proven, unexpected, real reason
- Match hook intensity to platform (LinkedIn = professional provocation, Instagram = scroll-stopping visual + text, X = sharp and punchy)

### Hook Formulas That Convert

**The Mistake:**
"The #1 mistake {audience} makes with {topic}..."
"Stop doing {common thing}. It's killing your {metric}."

**The Counter-Intuitive:**
"I stopped {common advice} and {unexpected positive result}."
"{Common belief} is wrong. Here's what actually works..."

**The Specific Number:**
"{Number} {things} that {benefit} (#{number} is a game-changer)"
"I spent {time/money} on {thing} so you don't have to. Here's what I learned."

**The Confession/Story:**
"3 years ago I {relatable bad situation}. Today I {transformation}."
"Nobody tells you this about {industry/topic}, but..."

**The Challenge:**
"Most {audience} can't {surprisingly hard thing}. Can you?"
"If you can't {simple-sounding thing}, your {strategy} is broken."

**The Hot Take:**
"{Unpopular opinion}: {bold statement about industry norm}"
"Controversial take: {thing everyone does} is a waste of {time/money}"

**The Question:**
"Why do {audience} keep {doing counterproductive thing}?"
"What if everything you know about {topic} is backwards?"

**The List Tease:**
"I analyzed {number} {things} and found {number} patterns that separate {winners} from {everyone else}:"
"The {topic} cheat sheet nobody asked for (but everyone needs):"

**The Social Proof:**
"{Result} in {timeframe}. Here's the exact playbook:"
"My client went from {before} to {after} using this {number}-step {process}:"

### Hook Variety Rule
No two consecutive posts should use the same hook formula. Across a monthly calendar, use at least 5 different hook types. Predictability kills engagement.

## Quality Checklist

Before presenting the calendar to the user, verify:

- [ ] Content pillars are balanced according to the defined mix
- [ ] No two consecutive posts on the same platform are the same type/pillar
- [ ] All key dates and seasonal moments from trend research are covered
- [ ] Posting times are optimized per platform
- [ ] Hooks use at least 5 different formulas across the month
- [ ] Every post has a clear strategic purpose (not filler)
- [ ] Visual direction is varied — no two posts look the same
- [ ] CTAs are varied — not every post asks for the same thing
- [ ] Caption length is appropriate per platform
- [ ] Hashtags are platform-specific and topic-specific (not the same set every post)
- [ ] The calendar is realistic given the client's posting frequency capacity
- [ ] Trend-informed content is included where relevant

Present the overview table first for user approval, then provide full drafts once the structure is approved.
