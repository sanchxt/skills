# Hashtag Strategy

## Purpose

Research and suggest relevant hashtags per platform and per post to maximize discoverability. Hashtags aren't the main growth lever — great content is. But the right hashtags put great content in front of the right people.

## Before Starting

1. Read `.growth/brand-profile.md` for industry, niche, and target audience
2. Read `.growth/competitors/` for competitor hashtag strategies
3. Read `.growth/voice-library.md` for any previously successful hashtag sets

## Hashtag Research Process

### 1. Build the Brand's Hashtag Universe

For each brand, create three tiers:

**Tier 1 — Branded (1-2 hashtags)**
- The brand's own hashtags: #BrandName, #BrandSlogan, #BrandCampaign
- These build brand recognition and make UGC discoverable
- Create if they don't exist yet

**Tier 2 — Niche (8-15 hashtags)**
- Specific to the brand's industry and niche
- Moderate competition: 10K-500K posts on Instagram
- High relevance to the target audience
- These are the workhorses — they put content in front of the right people

**Tier 3 — Broad (5-8 hashtags)**
- Higher competition: 500K-5M posts
- General industry or lifestyle hashtags
- Used for broader discovery — lower conversion but higher visibility
- Don't go too broad (e.g., #love with 2B posts is useless)

### 2. Research Methods

**From competitor analysis:**
- Pull the hashtags competitors use on their top-performing posts
- Note which competitors get more hashtag-driven reach
- Identify niche hashtags they've found that you should also use

**Via web search:**
- Search: `"best {industry} hashtags {current year}"`
- Search: `"trending {niche} hashtags"`
- Search: `"{platform} hashtags for {specific topic}"`

**Via Chrome browser (Instagram):**
- Visit hashtag pages on Instagram to check:
  - Total post count (gauge competition level)
  - Recent post quality (are real brands using it or just spam?)
  - Related hashtags (Instagram shows these — gold mine for discovery)

**Via Google Trends:**
- Check if any niche keywords are currently trending
- Trending keywords often make timely hashtags

### 3. Hashtag Evaluation Criteria

Not all hashtags are equal. Evaluate each one:

| Criteria | What to Check |
|----------|--------------|
| **Relevance** | Does this accurately describe the content and reach the target audience? |
| **Competition** | How many posts use it? (Sweet spot: 10K-500K for niche, 500K-5M for broad) |
| **Quality** | Are the posts using this hashtag high-quality brands or spam accounts? |
| **Recency** | Is this hashtag actively used or dead? Check most recent posts. |
| **Audience match** | Would the people browsing this hashtag be our target audience? |

**Red flags — avoid hashtags that:**
- Have been banned or flagged by the platform (shadow-ban risk)
- Are used primarily by spam/bot accounts
- Are overly generic (#love, #happy, #photooftheday)
- Have nothing to do with the content (hashtag stuffing)
- Are competitor branded hashtags (don't promote their brand)

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram
- **Quantity:** 5-15 per post (Instagram officially supports up to 30, but research shows 5-15 is optimal for reach)
- **Placement:** In the caption (not first comment — placement in first comment no longer helps and may hurt)
- **Mix per post:** 1 branded + 3-5 niche + 2-3 broad + 1-2 trending/seasonal (if relevant)
- **Rotation:** Don't use the same set every post. Prepare 3-4 hashtag sets per content pillar and rotate them. Using the same hashtags repeatedly can trigger algorithmic deprioritization.
- **Topic-specific:** Choose hashtags that match the SPECIFIC post topic, not just the brand's general niche

### X/Twitter
- **Quantity:** 1-3 maximum (more than 3 looks spammy on X)
- **Placement:** Integrated naturally into the tweet when possible, or at the end
- **Selection:** Only use a hashtag if it's genuinely trending or highly relevant
- **Trending:** Engage with trending hashtags ONLY when you have a genuinely relevant take — don't force it

### LinkedIn
- **Quantity:** 3-5 per post
- **Placement:** At the end of the post, after the CTA
- **Selection:** Mix industry hashtags (#DigitalMarketing, #SaaS) with topic hashtags (#ContentStrategy, #GrowthHacking)
- **Follow hashtags:** The brand should follow the hashtags it targets — this also helps content discovery

### Facebook
- **Quantity:** 1-3 or none (Facebook hashtags have minimal impact compared to other platforms)
- **When to use:** For campaigns, events, or branded content series
- **When to skip:** For regular organic posts — focus on content quality and shareability instead

## Per-Post Hashtag Selection

When creating content calendar entries, select hashtags that are specific to THAT post:

1. Start with 1 branded hashtag (if the brand has one)
2. Add 2-4 niche hashtags directly related to the post's specific topic (not the general brand niche)
3. Add 1-2 broader hashtags for wider discovery
4. Add 1 trending or seasonal hashtag if there's a genuinely relevant one right now
5. Add 1-2 from the competitor-inspired hashtag pool if relevant

**Example for a fitness brand posting about home workouts:**
- Branded: #FitWithBrandName
- Niche (topic-specific): #HomeWorkoutRoutine #BodyweightTraining #NoGymNeeded
- Broad: #FitnessMotivation #WorkoutFromHome
- Seasonal: #SummerFitness (if applicable)

**NOT this:** #fitness #gym #motivation #health #lifestyle #fitspo (too generic, not topic-specific)

Store these sets in the voice library (`.growth/voice-library.md`) under a "Hashtag Sets" section.

## Tracking & Optimization

During performance analysis:
1. Note which hashtag sets correlated with higher reach (Instagram shows hashtag-driven reach in Insights)
2. Identify hashtags that consistently appear on top-performing posts
3. Remove hashtags that don't contribute to reach
4. Update the hashtag sets quarterly — hashtag relevance and competition levels shift over time
5. Watch for newly trending niche hashtags to add to the rotation

## Seasonal & Campaign Hashtags

- For seasonal content, research timely hashtags in advance during trend research
- For brand campaigns, create a unique campaign hashtag and promote it across all content during the campaign
- For events or awareness days, use the official event hashtags (e.g., #WorldMentalHealthDay)
- After the campaign/season ends, remove these from rotation and archive in the voice library
