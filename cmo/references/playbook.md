# Eravue growth playbook: the first 90 days

**Written:** 2026-11-11. Re-validate monthly. Phases are a default, not a law: move faster if the numbers allow.

## What the research says (Nov 2026)

Sources (agency/vendor blogs, so treat them as practitioner consensus, not proof): [sellinger.ai](https://sellinger.ai/blog/ai-automation-agencies-get-clients), [aurozenmarketing.com](https://aurozenmarketing.com/how-to-get-first-clients-ai-automation-agency/), [locusfounder.com](https://locusfounder.com/guides/ai-automation-agency), [averi.ai](https://www.averi.ai/how-to/founder-led-content-on-linkedin-the-0-pipeline-channel-you-re-ignoring), [report.noticed.co](https://report.noticed.co/), [hothawk.ai](https://hothawk.ai/blog/deliverability/email-deliverability-news-2026), [instantly.ai](https://instantly.ai/blog/how-to-achieve-90-cold-email-deliverability-in-2025/?lng=en).

- The first 1-3 clients nearly always come from **people who already know or have seen the founders** (warm network, referrals, communities) and from **direct, personal outreach**, not from SEO or ads.
- **Narrow beats broad.** One niche, one problem, one small first step. A useful test: can the first message be written without the word "AI"?
- **A small paid first step converts**: a fixed-scope pilot, sprint or audit, then a monthly care/retainer.
- **One documented case study** is the asset that wins clients 2-10.
- **Timelines:** LinkedIn outbound gives a first meeting in about 1-2 weeks. Cold email takes 3-4 weeks (domain warm-up). Typical first client lands 30-90 days after outreach starts. Volume and speed shorten that.
- **LinkedIn founder content** works, but organic reach fell sharply in 2026. What still works: posts drawn from real client conversations, a consistent rhythm, and DMs following up on engagement.
- **Cold email** in 2026 needs SPF/DKIM/DMARC, a separate sending domain, warm-up, low volume per inbox, no open-tracking pixels and a visible opt-out.
- **India local:** Google Business Profile + WhatsApp are the core channels for local SMBs. In-person visits still work in Indian markets and are an unfair advantage for a Delhi NCR team.

## Positioning for the sprint

Lead with **outcomes for owner-led businesses that lose money to slow replies and manual work**: "every enquiry answered in under a minute and followed up until it books", "a fast site that turns visitors into WhatsApp enquiries", "reels edited every week". Eravue does many things; each message sells one.

**Beachheads for days 1-30** (50/50 India/international, the founders' decision). Re-rank after 3 weeks of data.

| Market | Beachhead | Why | Lead offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| IN | Interior designers and architects (NCR) | Emson Designs proof; high ticket per lead; Instagram-heavy | Website sprint, then WhatsApp lead follow-up |
| IN | Clinics (dental, derma, physio) and salons (NCR) | Lose calls and DMs daily; WhatsApp-run | WhatsApp/Instagram auto-reply + booking follow-up |

| Both | Agencies lacking AI or dev | One partner can bring many jobs | White-label build and automation |

## Phases

### Phase 0: Foundations (days 1-4)

Without these, outreach leaks. Do them in parallel with the first outreach, not instead of it.

1. **Tracking back on** (eravue-conversion): conversion events on every CTA, form, WhatsApp, phone and email link. The Nov 6 redesign removed them all.
2. **Offers approved** (eravue-sales proposes, the founders approve) → `offers.md` and `decisions.md`.
3. **Profiles that convert** (eravue-social §profiles): @eravuedotcom bio still says "Web & App | UI/UX | Brand | Growth Marketing | SEO", so align it to the AI-automation positioning. Also the founders' LinkedIn headlines and About sections, the company page, X, and highlights/pinned posts with proof.
4. **Google Business Profile tune-up** (eravue-conversion §local): categories, services, photos, posts, first reviews from past clients.
5. **Proof pack** (eravue-sales): 1-page case notes for each real past client (Emson, Bestor, DevLabs Alliance, Astro Shades, Caketastic, Yoop) and the 3 products, plus testimonial requests to past clients. A 2-3 minute demo video of an Eravue-built WhatsApp/AI receptionist flow to send in outreach.
6. **Warm list** (eravue-outreach warm mode): 100+ names from both founders' phones, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, college, ex-colleagues and family businesses, each tagged with who they could refer.
7. **Cold-email setup** if the founders approve the spend ([budget.md](budget.md)): secondary domain + 1-2 inboxes, warm-up starts day 1 because it takes 2-3 weeks.

### Phase 1: Cash sprint (weeks 1-4). Goal: first paid client(s).

Full-time quotas for two founders once ramped (week 1 is lighter while profiles and offers settle). These are **touches**: a personal, specific message to a qualified person.

| Stream | Per day | Owner skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm network + referral asks | 10 (week 1-2), then 3 | eravue-outreach (warm) | Voice notes and calls beat text. Ask "who do you know", not "do you need" |
| Inbound replies (Reddit, X, LinkedIn posts, FB groups, forums) | 8-12 | eravue-outreach (hunt) | Fresh posts only; rules respected |
| Upwork / marketplaces | 3-5 proposals | eravue-outreach (draft) | Needs the profile live; connects cost money |
| Instagram DMs (IN beachheads, some INTL) | ramp 5 → 20 | eravue-instagram | Never above its cap |
| LinkedIn connection notes + DMs (INTL + IN B2B) | 15-20 | eravue-outreach + eravue-social | Engage with their posts first when possible |
| Cold email (INTL) | 0 until warmed, then 20-30 | eravue-outreach | Plain text, no tracking pixel, opt-out line |
| Loom/video audits for score-5 leads | 2-3 | eravue-outreach + eravue-sales | Highest reply rate; worth the 10 minutes |
| In-person visits (NCR clusters: a market of clinics/salons/studios) | 1-2 days/week, 8-10 visits | eravue-outreach (prospect) | Bring a printed 1-page gap audit |
| Partners (agencies, freelancers, web designers) | 3 | eravue-outreach (partner) | White-label offer |
| Content | LinkedIn 1/day (alternating founders), X 2-3/day, reels 3/week, @eravuedotcom 3/week | eravue-social, lifewithcofounders | Content supports outreach: proof, demos, build-in-public |

**Weekly activity target:** ~250 touches → expect ~15 replies → 5 calls → 2 proposals → aim for the first win in week 2-4. If week 2 ends with fewer than 8 replies, the targeting or message is wrong. Fix that before adding volume.

### Phase 2: Proof and retainers (weeks 5-8)

- Every win → testimonial, case study page, GBP/Clutch review, before/after reel, referral ask (eravue-sales §after the win).
- Every one-off project → monthly care/retainer offer at delivery.
- Cut the bottom channels; give their time to the top two by calls booked.
- Start a lead magnet built from real work (e.g. "Missed-lead audit": we test how fast you reply to an enquiry and send a 1-page report).

### Phase 3: Scale what works (weeks 9-13)

- Productize the best-selling offer with its own landing page (eravue-conversion) and bottom-of-funnel SEO page (seo).
- Small paid tests from the budget: boost the best-performing proof reel, or a Meta click-to-WhatsApp ad in one NCR niche.
- Formal partner/referral program (a referral fee for anyone who brings a client).
- Hire or contract help for delivery if retainers grow (protect delivery quality: churn kills MRR).

Activity targets matter more early: missing an outcome target with full activity means change the message or offer. Missing it with low activity means do the work.

## Anti-goals for the sprint

- No big SEO content programme (maintenance only: fix indexing, 1 bottom-of-funnel post a week max).
- No new website redesigns. Conversion fixes only.
- No new products. Sell services; the products are proof.
- No vanity growth (follower hacks, engagement pods, giveaways).
