# Experiments

Growth is a series of small bets with a kill date. One running experiment per channel, so results aren't muddled.

## Choosing what to test

Score ideas with ICE (Impact on calls/revenue, Confidence from evidence, Ease in founder-hours), each 1-5. Run the top one per channel. Prefer tests that change **who** you contact or **what** you offer over tests that change wording. Wording tests need volume Eravue doesn't have yet.

## Log format (`.growth/experiments.md`, newest first)

```markdown
## E007 · Instagram · Free 1-page site mockup vs plain question opener
- **Status:** running (started 2026-11-14, judge on 2026-11-28)
- **Hypothesis:** Interior designers reply more when the first DM offers a concrete mockup.
- **Setup:** Alternate openers A/B across the next 60 Instagram DMs to IN interior designers; tag leads with angle=A|B in the tracker.
- **Success metric:** reply rate and calls booked; needs ≥30 sends per arm to judge.
- **Cost:** ~10 min extra per B lead.
- **Result:** (fill in)
- **Decision:** scale / iterate / kill, and why.
```

## Judging

- Judge only at the planned date or sample size. Don't peek-and-switch daily.
- With small samples, look for big differences (2x and more). Treat anything smaller as "no difference" and keep the cheaper option.
- Record the decision and carry the learning into `strategy.md` §Learnings and the owning skill's references (e.g. eravue-instagram `dm-playbook.md`, lifewithcofounders `log.md`).
- A failed experiment with a clear lesson is a good outcome. An experiment with no decision is waste.
