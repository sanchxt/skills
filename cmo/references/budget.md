# Budget: ₹5,000-7,000 per month (founders' decision, 2026-11-11)

Money is scarce, founder time isn't (they're full-time). Spend only where money buys something time can't: deliverability, access to buyers, or reach for an already-proven post.

## Default allocation (verify live prices before proposing; they change)

| Item | Rough monthly | Why | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold-email sending domain (e.g. a `.co`/`.in` lookalike of eravue) + 1-2 mailboxes (Zoho Mail / Google Workspace starter tier) | ₹500-1,500 | Protects eravue.com's reputation; needed for INTL cold email | Phase 0, start warm-up immediately |
| Upwork Connects | ₹1,000-2,000 | Access to buyers who are hiring right now | Phase 1, once the profile is live |
| Small boost of a proven reel / click-to-WhatsApp test | ₹1,500-3,000 | Reach for content that already converts organically | Only after a post beats the account median on saves/DMs, or Phase 3 |
| Reserve | ₹1,000 | Printing audits for visits, a domain renewal, a tool trial | As needed |

Free tools to prefer: Gmail/WhatsApp Business app, Loom free tier (or OBS/screen recording), Canva free, Umami (already self-hosted), Search Console, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn free, Buffer-style free schedulers if posting via browser fails.

## Rules

- **Ask before every spend** with: item, vendor, exact price (checked today), what it should produce, and how we'll judge it. Record the approval in `decisions.md` and the spend in `metrics.csv` (`ops.spend_inr`, note = item).
- You never pay or enter card details. The founders make the purchase.
- No annual plans during the sprint unless the monthly price is much worse and the tool is already proven.
- Review spend against results monthly: cost per call booked, by channel.
