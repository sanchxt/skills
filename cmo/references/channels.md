# Channel map

For each channel: who owns it, what "working" looks like, and when to cut it. Re-rank monthly from `leads.py stats` (calls booked per founder-hour is the metric that decides).

| Channel | Owner skill | Speed to cash | Leading indicator (healthy) | Cut or fix if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm network + referrals | eravue-outreach (warm) | Days | ≥20% reply, any intro | Never cut; drop to maintenance after the list is worked |
| Reddit / X / forum inbound | eravue-outreach (hunt) | Days | ≥10% reply on hiring/recommendation posts | <3% reply after 60 replies → change subs/queries |
| Upwork / marketplaces | eravue-outreach (draft) | 1-3 weeks | ≥1 interview per 15 proposals | 0 interviews after 40 proposals → rewrite profile and offer |
| Instagram DMs | eravue-instagram | 1-3 weeks | ≥8% reply | <3% after 100 DMs → change niche or opener; any block warning → stop for a week |
| LinkedIn outreach | eravue-outreach + eravue-social | 1-2 weeks | ≥30% accept, ≥8% reply | <15% accept → fix profile/targeting |
| Cold email (INTL) | eravue-outreach | 3-4 weeks (warm-up) | ≥3% positive reply, <2% bounce | Bounce >3% or spam complaints → stop sending, fix list/auth |

## Founder time is the real budget

Two founders at ~8 hours/day is roughly 80 hours a week. Rough default split in the cash sprint:
- 45% direct outreach and follow-ups (sending, voice notes, visits)
- 20% sales calls, proposals, demos
- 20% content (recording reels, LinkedIn posts, commenting)
- 10% delivery for won clients (rises as clients land: never let delivery slip)
- 5% foundations, reviews and planning

Re-balance weekly in the plan. When the first clients arrive, delivery quality beats new outreach: a happy client is the case study, the referral and the retainer.
