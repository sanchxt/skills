# Link Building Strategy

## Purpose

Build sustainable authority through ethical backlinks, digital PR, useful assets, partnerships, and internal linking. Avoid spam, PBNs, automated link schemes, irrelevant directories, and paid links that violate search guidelines.

## Before Starting

1. Read `.seo/project-profile.md`.
2. Read `.seo/competitors/`, `.seo/content/`, and `.seo/links/` if present.
3. Identify the pages that deserve links: tools, research, guides, templates, studies, product pages, local pages, or linkable assets.
4. Research current competitor link patterns and prospect quality.

## Strategy Areas

- **Backlink gap:** sites linking to competitors but not us.
- **Digital PR:** expert commentary, data stories, reports, media mentions, podcasts, events.
- **Guest contributions:** legitimate editorial sites with topical relevance.
- **Resource pages/directories:** curated, maintained, niche-relevant resources.
- **Broken link building:** replace dead resources with better current content.
- **Unlinked mentions:** ask for attribution where brand is already mentioned.
- **Partnerships:** vendors, customers, integrations, associations, communities.
- **Internal links:** fastest authority distribution lever under direct control.

## Prospect Quality Filters

Pursue a prospect only when it passes most of these:

- Topically relevant to the site and target page.
- Real audience and editorial standards.
- Indexable, maintained, and not obviously spammy.
- Link would be contextually useful to readers.
- Not part of a link farm, PBN, or paid placement scheme.
- Reasonable authority compared with effort.
